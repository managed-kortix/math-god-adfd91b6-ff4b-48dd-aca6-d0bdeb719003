# Cycle 112: strategic rotation to Navier phase transversality

The portfolio was reranked after the Hodge semiregularity route met its full
applicability gate.  Navier--Stokes is selected for the next bounded main funnel
because it retains an equation-level, non-equivalent production question:
whether full Fourier dynamics quantitatively ejects coherent stretching packets
from maximizing phase configurations.

The exact next lemma is not the abstract hypergraph statement already proved.
It asks for an explicit real divergence-free trigonometric polynomial whose
active triads begin at simultaneous maximizing phases, have positive total
vortex stretching, and whose **full unprojected** Euler phase velocity is
quantitatively transverse after every first-order generated mode is included.
The first experiment must either produce such an integer-wavevector packet or
prove a structural obstruction to coupling phase escape with positive
stretching.  A Galerkin-only abstract coefficient model does not pass.

## Bounded scouts

- **BSD.** Higher `2^m` descent plus the Cassels--Tate pairing gives an exact
  finite stopping certificate for `Sha[2^infty]`: the pairing radical vanishes
  exactly when the tower has terminated.  It does not compare that algebraic
  order with the complex leading coefficient and has no uniform stopping level.
- **Navier analyticity reserve.** The Gevrey inequality contains a cubic
  supercritical norm term.  Energy controls only unweighted integrated
  enstrophy; scaling and short high-frequency packets defeat any inference of a
  uniform analyticity radius or determining-mode bound.
- **RH.** Effective Jensen hyperbolicity currently reaches a logarithmic
  degree/shift window.  A polynomial window such as `d=o(n^(1/3))` would require
  new degree-uniform saddle and root-stability estimates.  Fixed-degree
  asymptotics plus finite checking cannot cover the unbounded low-shift degree
  direction.  A reported numerical gap in a recent effective exponent requires
  primary-source author-level verification before being treated as a claim.
- **P versus NP.** A subpolynomial clean-space lower bound for a square-padded
  SAT problem would imply `P!=NP`, but catalytic restoration stores history in
  the catalyst at intermediate times.  Endpoint restoration gives no
  unrestricted information bottleneck.
- **Yang--Mills.** Planar massiveness does not descend to finite `N` without
  exponentially weighted, separation-uniform control of every genus correction
  and then separate control of small ranks and continuum limits.
- **Hodge.** The rank-`20` lemma remains bounded internal work and does not
  warrant author contact or publication action without a solved case or a
  stronger accepted result.

No Millennium result is claimed.
