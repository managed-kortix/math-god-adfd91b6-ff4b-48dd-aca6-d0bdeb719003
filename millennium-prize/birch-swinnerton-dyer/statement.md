# Official statement: Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer

Primary source checked 2026-07-25: Andrew Wiles,
<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/birchswin.pdf>.

For every elliptic curve `E/Q`,
`ord_(s=1) L(E,s) = rank_Z E(Q)`. The refined leading-coefficient formula and
finiteness of Sha are stronger than the displayed official rank assertion.
Finite verification or a result for a family is not a solution.
