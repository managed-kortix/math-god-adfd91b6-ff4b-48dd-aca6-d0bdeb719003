# Cycle 136: certificate transport and explicit localization for 433a1

The Cycle 135 Kurihara certificate admits two exact refinements: its weighted
value depends only on the mixed rectangle quotient of the grouped modular-
symbol matrix, and the two rational generators have an invertible localization
matrix at the auxiliary primes.  These are robustness and self-containment
checks, not a new BSD theorem.

For a matrix `M=(M_ij)` indexed by `F_p x F_p`, define

\[
\mathcal R(M)=\sum_{i,j\in\mathbf F_p}ijM_{ij}
\]

and

\[
M^{\square}_{ij}=M_{ij}-M_{i0}-M_{0j}+M_{00}.
\]

Since `sum_(t in F_p)t=0`,

\[
\mathcal R(M^\square)=\mathcal R(M).
\]

More generally, if at the grouped-matrix level

\[
M'_{ij}=uM_{\alpha i,\beta j}+A(i)+B(j),
\qquad u,\alpha,\beta\ne0,
\]

then

\[
\mathcal R(M')=u\alpha^{-1}\beta^{-1}\mathcal R(M).
\]

Thus nonvanishing is insensitive to row/column noise and primitive-root
relabeling.  If such a relation is derived before grouping, constant fiber size
must be checked.  For `29*113` at `p=7`, every discrete-log rectangle contains
exactly `4*16=64` units, so the passage is valid.

For the Cycle 135 matrix,

\[
\mathcal R(M)=\mathcal R(M^\square)=3\pmod7.
\]

Now let

\[
P=(0,1),\qquad Q=(-1,1)
\]

on `E: y^2+xy=x^3+1`.  Exact finite-field arithmetic gives

\[
[Q]=5[P]\quad\text{in }E(\mathbf F_{29})/7,
\]

\[
[Q]=4[P]\quad\text{in }E(\mathbf F_{113})/7.
\]

With local bases generated by `[P]`, the localization matrix is

\[
L=\begin{pmatrix}1&5\\1&4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det L=-1=6\ne0\pmod7.
\]

Explicit divisibility witnesses are

\[
Q-5P=(5,9)=7(1,1)\quad\text{over }\mathbf F_{29},
\]

\[
Q-4P=(88,51)=7(7,32)\quad\text{over }\mathbf F_{113}.
\]

The nonzero determinant independently proves that `P,Q` are independent modulo
seven and hence rationally independent.  Combined with the Selmer upper bound
from the Cycle 135 Kurihara value and Kim's theorem, this sandwiches the rank at
two without a separate database or descent rank determination.  The determinant
alone does not prove that the full Selmer group equals the rational Kummer
subspace: the upper bound and
`Sha[7^infty]=0` still use Kim's theorem and the Kurihara nonvanishing.

The rectangle observation is elementary certificate bookkeeping.  Under
prime-to-`p` isogenies it transports nonvanishing only after period, Manin, and
integrality factors are verified to be `p`-units.  No novelty is claimed for
the transport principle itself.
