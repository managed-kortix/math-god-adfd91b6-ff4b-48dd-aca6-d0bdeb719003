# Cycle 186: cyclotomic modulus minimality audit

## Verdict

The field

\[
 L_0\mathbf Q(\zeta_{8\cdot7\cdot433\cdot29})
\]

is a mathematically valid refinement for a same-Frobenius collision test, but
it is neither a governing field for `c(q,29)` nor a minimal field for the
stated fixed-`ell` experiment. Equality of Frobenius in this compositum implies
equality in `L_0`, so a zero/nonzero collision there still refutes governance by
`L_0`. The converse loss is substantial: the full cyclotomic field splits one
`L_0`-Frobenius fibre according to residue data that the current admissibility
predicate does not use, making collisions needlessly rarer.

If admissibility is checked separately for each candidate prime, no
cyclotomic enlargement is logically necessary: same conjugacy class in `L_0`
and different zero/nonzero values of `c(q,29)` already give the desired
counterexample on the admissible packet. If admissibility is to be automatic on
each search fibre, the correct enlargement is the abelian character field
generated by the predicates actually declared in the packet, not the full
cyclotomic field of their conductors.

## The fixed auxiliary-prime condition

Here `#E(F_29)=28`, so `a_29(E)=2`, while `29+1=30=2 mod 7`. Consequently

\[
 a_{29}(E^{(D_q)})\equiv30\pmod 7
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \left(\frac{D_q}{29}\right)=1.
\]

Because `29=1 mod 4` and `D_q` is `q` or `-q`, with the sign chosen by the
fundamental-discriminant convention,

\[
 \left(\frac{D_q}{29}\right)=\left(\frac q{29}\right).
\]

Thus the auxiliary-prime admissibility test uses only the quadratic character
modulo `29`. It is governed by the quadratic subfield
`Q(sqrt(29))`, not by all of `Q(zeta_29)`. Fixing `q mod 29` imposes 28
cyclotomic classes where the predicate sees only two; relative to the quadratic
field, it can make a pair collision about 14 times harder before any other
constraints are counted.

The Cycle 185 note incorrectly records the anchor character as `-1`. In fact
`1289=13 mod 29` and `10^2=13 mod 29`, so `(1289/29)=1`. Consequently that
progression is admissible at fixed `ell=29`. It remains a highly nonminimal
refinement: fixing a full residue modulo 29 splits the admissible quadratic
character fiber into 14 separate cyclotomic fibers.

The definition of `c(q,29)` itself supplies no reason to adjoin
`Q(zeta_29)`. The discrete logarithms are taken in the fixed group
`(Z/29Z)^x`; the varying input is the modular-symbol line of the twist of
conductor `433q^2`. Adjoining a cyclotomic field therefore does not prove, or
even formally suggest, that `c(q,29)` is Frobenian.

## Audit of the four factors

- **The factor `7` is redundant in the compositum.** The Weil pairing gives
  `Q(zeta_7) subset Q(E[7]) subset L_0`. Hence
  `L_0 Q(zeta_(8*7*433*29)) = L_0 Q(zeta_(8*433*29))`.
  Retaining `7` in a displayed modulus is harmless bookkeeping, but it is not
  field-minimal and adds no Frobenius condition beyond `L_0`.
- **The factor `29` is overpowered.** The presently stated one-prime
  admissibility condition sees only `(q/29)`, so `Q(sqrt(29))` suffices. Full
  residue equality modulo `29` has no stated logical role.
- **The factor `433` is overpowered unless a full residue class is explicitly
  required.** Quadratic-twist local data at the multiplicative prime sees a
  quadratic character such as `(D_q/433)`, hence at most a quadratic subfield
  for that predicate. The usual twist root-number formula
  `w(E^(D_q))=w(E)*(D_q/-433)` is itself one quadratic character; it does not
  require all of `Q(zeta_433)`.
- **The factor `8` depends on the packet.** Fixing the fundamental-discriminant
  sign requires at most `q mod 4`. If the local condition at `2` also fixes
  `(D_q/2)`, then `q mod 8` is relevant and `Q(zeta_8)` is a reasonable
  encoding. If neither residue class nor the separate `2`-adic character is a
  declared predicate, the factor `8` is an unnecessary restriction.

The exact minimal character field cannot be named until the optional packet
conditions in Cycle 182 are replaced by one explicit predicate. Root number,
sign, and separate local characters can also be products of one another, so
adjoining a separate cyclotomic field for every conductor can duplicate
information even after each factor has been reduced to a quadratic subfield.

## Intersections with `L_0`

The unconditional intersection needed here is

\[
 \mathbf Q(\zeta_7)\subset L_0.
\]

For `29`, the Kummer field is unramified at `29`, whereas every nontrivial
subfield of `Q(zeta_29)` is ramified there. Thus

\[
 L_0\cap\mathbf Q(\zeta_{29})=\mathbf Q.
\]

Under the maximal semidirect-product model used in Cycle 184,

\[
 \operatorname{Gal}(L_0/\mathbf Q)
 \simeq (\mathbf F_7^2)^2\rtimes\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbf F_7),
\]

the translation module has no nonzero coinvariants and the abelianization is
the determinant quotient `F_7^x`. Accordingly the abelian subextensions of
`L_0` lie in `Q(zeta_7)`. This gives trivial intersections with the prime-to-7
cyclotomic factors `Q(zeta_8)`, `Q(zeta_29)`, and `Q(zeta_433)` in that maximal
model. If the actual Galois image is smaller, these intersections must be
computed in the certified actual subgroup; they must not be inferred from the
maximal model.

Numerically, the displayed cyclotomic field has degree

\[
 \varphi(8)\varphi(7)\varphi(433)\varphi(29)=290304.
\]

After the already-contained `7` factor is removed, the maximal possible new
degree is `4*432*28=48384`. By contrast, the fixed-`29` admissibility condition
alone adds degree two. Even a packet retaining independent mod-`8`, quadratic
`433`, and quadratic `29` data adds at most `4*2*2=16` before intersections.
Thus the full choice can refine the relevant character fibres by a factor of
`3024` in that comparison, with no corresponding logical gain.

## Recommended collision criterion

Use one of the following, in increasing order of automatic filtering.

1. Search same-conjugacy-class pairs in `L_0`, check every admissibility
   condition directly for both primes, and compare only zero versus nonzero
   `c(q,29)`. This is the logically minimal test of `L_0`-governance.
2. Freeze the exact admissibility predicate, express each of its prime-varying
   clauses as a Dirichlet character, and adjoin only the field cut out by those
   characters. This makes admissibility constant on fibres without fixing
   irrelevant residue classes.
3. Use the full modulus only as an implementation convenience, while stating
   explicitly that it is a sufficient nonminimal refinement and reporting the
   collision-density penalty. A collision remains valid; a collision-free
   search is materially weaker because of the artificially smaller fibres.

No finite cyclotomic enlargement resolves the central governance question for
`c(q,29)`: that coordinate still comes from varying conductor `433q^2`. The
cyclotomic factor can govern declared congruence filters, not the modular-symbol
coordinate whose failure of `L_0`-governance is being tested.

No BSD case or finite-governance theorem is claimed.
