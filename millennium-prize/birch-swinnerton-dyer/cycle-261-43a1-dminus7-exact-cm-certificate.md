# Cycle 261: exact `D=-7` CM certificate for `43a1`

## Result

Let

\[
 E:y^2+y=x^3+x^2,
 \qquad P=(0,0),
\]

and let `pi:X_0(43)->E` be the strong Weil parametrization normalized by
`pi(infinity)=O` and by pullback of the Neron differential to the normalized
newform differential. For the Heegner point

\[
 \tau={-37+\sqrt{-7}\over86},
\]

the exact algebraic calculation below proves

\[
 \pi(\tau)=P. \tag{261.1}
\]

Since `h(-7)=1`, the Hilbert class field is `K=Q(sqrt(-7))`; hence the literal
trace defining the maximal-order Heegner point has one summand and

\[
 y_K=P. \tag{261.2}
\]

This closes the first terminal gap in Cycle 260. It does **not** by itself
prove `Sha(43a1/Q)=0`; the residual-image, Mordell--Weil saturation, and
2-descent requirements listed there are separate inputs. The later
`43a1/K-mordell-weil-certificate.md` proves the required literal equality
`E(K)_free=ZP`, rather than only a statement over `Q`.

## Algebraic CM input

The order of discriminant `-7` has class number one and Hilbert class
polynomial

\[
 H_{-7}(T)=T+3375.
\]

The equality `37^2+7=4*43*8` gives the cyclic `43`-isogeny represented by the
chosen point of `X_0(43)`: `tau` is the root in the upper half-plane of the
primitive form `[43,37,8]`, while `43*tau` is the root of `[1,37,344]`.
The verifier reduces both forms exactly to `[1,1,2]`. Thus both elliptic curves
in that isogeny have CM by the same maximal order and, since its class number is
one, at this point

\[
 j(\tau)=j(43\tau)=-3375. \tag{261.3}
\]

Equivalently, for the two Fricke-invariant functions

\[
 S=j(z)+j(43z),\qquad R=j(z)j(43z),
\]

the CM point satisfies

\[
 S=-6750,\qquad R=3375^2=11390625. \tag{261.4}
\]

## Exact functions on the optimal quotient

PARI's `elltaniyama` gives the formal Laurent series `X(q),Y(q)` for the
normalized optimal parametrization. They satisfy exactly

\[
 Y^2+Y=X^3+X^2,
\]

and begin `X=q^-2+2q^-1+...`, `Y=-q^-3-3q^-2+...`. The verifier checks the
exact differential normalization

\[
 {qX'(q)\over 2Y(q)+1}=f(q),
\]

so the sign is not inferred from the Weierstrass equation or a point
recognition. It separately applies PARI's Atkin--Lehner operator and checks
`f|W_43=f`. Hence `pi o W_43-pi` is constant. Its value is the image of the
other rational cusp; `E(Q)_tors=0`, checked exactly, so the constant is zero.
The modular degree is two, and therefore the map is the Fricke quotient. Thus
`S` and `R` descend to rational functions on `E`. These checks also rule out
the possible negation or torsion-translation normalization of `pi`.

Their only poles are at the image `O` of the two cusps. At either cusp the two
orders for `j(z),j(43z)` are `1,43`; the cusps are exchanged rather than fixed
by Fricke, so the quotient is unramified there. Consequently `S` has pole
order at most `43`, while `R` has pole order `44`. The checked leading terms
`ord_O(X)=-2`, `ord_O(Y)=-3` give the exact Riemann--Roch bases

\[
 L(43O)=\langle1,X,\ldots,X^{21},Y,XY,\ldots,X^{20}Y\rangle,
\]

\[
 L(44O)=\langle1,X,\ldots,X^{22},Y,XY,\ldots,X^{20}Y\rangle.
\]

The script computes `j=E4^3/Delta` as a formal Laurent series over `Q` and
solves for the coordinates of `S` and `R` using only coefficients through the
constant term. In a separate pass it checks all coefficients through `q^43`
and `q^44`, respectively, with an explicit precision guard. This is an
identity proof: after cancellation of the poles, a nonzero member of `L(mO)`
cannot vanish to order greater than `m` at the non-pole cusp. No floating-point
modular integration, point recognition, elliptic logarithm, or BSD quotient
enters.

Writing

\[
 S=A_S(X)+B_S(X)Y,\qquad R=A_R(X)+B_R(X)Y,
\]

the exact recovered functions have the decisive values

\[
 A_S(0)=-6750,\quad B_S(0)=-22750,
 \qquad A_R(0)=11390625. \tag{261.5}
\]

The full coefficient vectors are deterministically regenerated by
`43a1/verify_dminus7_cm_exact.gp`; retaining large copied polynomials in prose
would add transcription risk rather than proof value.

## Exact fiber elimination

Set

\[
 F=Y^2+Y-X^3-X^2,
\quad G_S=A_S(X)+B_S(X)Y+6750,
\quad G_R=A_R(X)+B_R(X)Y-11390625.
\]

The script computes the three exact rational/integer resultants

\[
 \operatorname{Res}_Y(F,G_S),\quad
 \operatorname{Res}_Y(F,G_R),\quad
 \operatorname{Res}_Y(G_S,G_R)
\]

and first rejects a zero resultant, then monic-normalizes their explicit
polynomial gcd and verifies that it is exactly `X`. Resultants can contain
projection factors caused by degree drops, but such factors can only enlarge
an individual zero set: every genuine common affine point still zeros all
three resultants. Taking the exact gcd therefore avoids relying on any one
possibly extraneous factor. Consequently every point
of `E` satisfying both CM equations (261.4) has `X=0`. On the fiber `X=0`,
the curve equation gives `Y=0` or `Y=-1`. Equation `G_S=0`, together with
`B_S(0)=-22750`, selects `Y=0` and rejects `Y=-1`. The unique point is
therefore `(0,0)`, proving (261.1).
