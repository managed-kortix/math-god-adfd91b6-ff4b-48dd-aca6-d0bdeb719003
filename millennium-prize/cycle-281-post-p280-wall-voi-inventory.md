does not assign a restriction-independent scale or owner to a physical gate.
One gate can be the SAT-active first merge under restrictions at arbitrarily
many dyadic scales, including on an explicit coordinate subcube of the frozen
padded `3SAT` function. Consequently the packet cannot derive the global
cross-scale disjointness needed to sum `cN` gates over `eta log N` scales.

This is a wall at the packet's first missing theorem: a cross-scale ownership or
anti-sharing result for every unrestricted circuit computing the frozen

prove an unrestricted-circuit lower bound, or prove `P!=NP`. The admitted proof
interface is terminal. No repair, alternate scale definition, ownership search,
or replacement packet is authorized.
