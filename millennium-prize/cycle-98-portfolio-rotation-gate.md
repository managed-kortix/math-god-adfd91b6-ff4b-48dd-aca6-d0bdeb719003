## BSD: adelic determinant lines

The Bloch--Kato fundamental line packages Mordell--Weil, local conditions,
periods, regulators, Tamagawa factors, and Sha.  A product formula would become
refined BSD only after constructing one rational integral zeta element whose
archimedean realization is
\[
 L^{(r)}(E,1)/(r!\Omega_E R_\infty)
\]
and whose `p`-adic realizations are the normalized `p`-adic leading terms divided
by their regulators.  Arithmetic Riemann--Roch compares geometric determinant
metrics but does not construct this motivic zeta element or identify the two
regulator realizations.  The missing compatibility is essentially ETNC/refined
BSD, so the product formula is organizational rather than productive.

## Hodge: coniveau induction

Alterations resolve a support after it is known.  They do not show that a
primitive Hodge class lies in `N^1`.  Assuming Hodge in lower dimensions,
\[
 \operatorname{Hdg}^{2p}(X)\subseteq N^1H^{2p}(X)
\]
is equivalent to Hodge for `X`: one direction is support of an algebraic cycle,
and the other pushes a lower-dimensional Hodge cycle through a resolved divisor.
Full `N^p` membership in degree `2p` is directly algebraicity.  A diagonal or
primitive-projector decomposition that factors through lower dimension already
asserts the missing geometric coniveau.

## Navier--Stokes: pressure-Hessian depletion

With `S` the strain, `H=Hess p`, and `q=omega.S omega`, the exact identity is
\[
 D_tq=|S\omega|^2-\omega\cdot H\omega
 +\nu\bigl(2\Delta\omega\cdot S\omega+
 \omega\cdot(\Delta S)\omega\bigr).
\]
The quartic vorticity term cancels.  The pressure Poisson equation fixes only
`tr H`; at fixed trace the directional value `omega.H omega` can be arbitrary.
An exact stationary affine Euler/Navier field has
\[
 S=\operatorname{diag}(a,-a,0),\quad \omega=(0,0,w),
\]
and full pressure exactly cancels the positive production predicted by the
restricted-Euler isotropic closure.  This is a local-mechanism counterexample,
not a finite-energy Clay solution.  A successful theorem must use the complete
nonlocal pressure compatibility.

## P versus NP: general Circuit-SAT speedup

Williams proves that one uniform exact general Circuit-SAT algorithm running in
\[
 2^n\operatorname{poly}(m)/n^{f(n)},\qquad f(n)\to\infty,
\]
for every polynomial circuit size implies `NEXP not subset P/poly`.  A fixed
polynomial saving does not suffice.  This major lower bound is compatible with
`P=NP`, so it does not reach the Clay separation and is retained only as an
algorithm-to-lower-bound calibration.

## Yang--Mills: loop equations

Finite-lattice Schwinger--Dyson equations plus complete positivity determine the
already unique finite Gibbs state.  Truncated loop systems do not.  Reflection
positivity yields positive transfer-matrix spectral measures, but uniqueness
does not exclude weight arbitrarily near zero energy.  An area law controls the
external-charge sector, not vacuum-sector glueball excitations.  A mass gap
requires a volume- and cutoff-uniform connected clustering or transfer-matrix
inequality on a dense gauge-invariant observable algebra.
