# Cycle 235: boundary repair and continuum stop rule

## Strategic verdict

Further boundary-polymer work has positive but strictly bounded expected value.
A correct repair would turn the Cycle 232 bulk constants and Cycle 233 cyclic
exponent into an explicit full-Hilbert strong-coupling theorem and would settle
the exact scope of Yarotsky's endpoint insertion.  It would not, by itself,
advance the continuum Clay quantifiers: in the present normalization the
certificate is at extremely small `lambda`, whereas the continuum trajectory
has `lambda=2/g^4 -> infinity`.

Accordingly Yang--Mills is not rotated merely because Cycle 234 failed.  One
frozen repair checkpoint below must first receive a terminal written audit.
Passing that checkpoint is worth preserving as a bounded theorem, but continued
ownership of the Millennium main funnel additionally requires the separate
continuum-admission packet in Section 3.

## 1. Frozen boundary-renewal checkpoint `BR235`

**Terminal status: `PASS`.** This status is assigned only after equations
(235.6)--(235.8) are typed by the boundary profile
`C,H_-^*,H_+,Hom(H_-,H_+)`, sector pairs are direct-summed, separate marks use
the explicit outer product, and endpoint vectors enter through evaluation.
The augmented-component construction supplies the five items below, while the
companion endpoint audit proves from Yarotsky's exact `I/J` resolution that the
complete tensor graph has no omitted endpoint wire or shared constraint. Bare
propagation is a connected string of `J` events, not an omitted channel. See
`cycle-235-augmented-boundary-graph.md` and
`cycle-235-endpoint-completeness-audit.md`.

The only boundary repair funded before the next portfolio decision was the
canonical temporal-renewal architecture.  Starting from the exact Cycle 232
event expansion, assign every bulk connected component its closed time
projection.  Use the ordered union of those projections and the endpoint
sectors to cut at every complete vacuum time layer and to form maximal renewal
blocks.  Temporally interleaved but spatially disjoint components must be
handled by this rule rather than being declared connected.

`BR235` is `PASS` only if one manuscript proves all of the following.

1. **Exact identity.** For each finite periodic spatial volume, Euclidean
   length `N`, endpoint sectors `A,B`, and endpoint vectors in those sectors,
   give a canonical map from every labelled event configuration to an ordered
   family of ordinary and endpoint renewal objects.  Give an inverse or an
   exact multiplicity formula and prove equality of the resulting partition
   function or matrix element, not only an inequality.
2. **Entangled endpoints.** Define activities after summing all labels hidden
   by the renewal map.  The definition must cover arbitrary vectors in
   `ran Q_A` and `ran Q_B`; no spatial factorization of an endpoint vector may
   be assumed.
3. **Uniform tilted activity.** Prove an absolute summed-activity estimate with
   an insertion radius `r_Lambda>0` independent of `N` and with every
   `theta<log(3/e)`.  Constants may depend on the finite spatial volume.  A
   bound for each formal summand, or a radius or tilt that deteriorates with
   `N`, does not pass.
4. **Separated faces.** Derive an `N`-independent one-face logarithmic term and
   a two-face cluster remainder `O(exp(-delta N))` for every
   `delta<theta`.  The proof must include clusters containing separate initial
   and final marks connected through ordinary objects, not only one object
   marked at both faces.
5. **Spectral closure.** Reproduce the open-ball spectral argument on the full
   untruncated ambient Hilbert space, transport uniqueness along the bounded
   coupling segment, and then restrict by the commuting Haar Gauss projector.
   An independent hostile audit must verify that the conclusion is full-space,
   volume-uniform in exponent, and no stronger than the stated finite-lattice
   theorem.

`BR235` is `FAIL` if an exact finite configuration or family disproves the
renewal identity, the required summed-activity estimate, or same-tilt
one/two-face separation.  It is `WALL` if a complete written reduction shows
that the remaining estimate is the arbitrary-boundary gap statement itself,
with no weaker independently testable lemma.  `OPEN`, partial calculations,
or another informal fusion proposal are not terminal statuses.

No portfolio rotation occurs while this frozen checkpoint lacks a terminal
`PASS`, `FAIL`, or `WALL` audit.  Conversely, a terminal audit ends funding for
further variants of this boundary-polymer architecture unless materially new
input changes one of the five tests.

## 2. Value assigned at the checkpoint

A `BR235 PASS` records the explicit strong-coupling theorem and its constants.
It does not count as a continuum bridge and does not automatically retain
Yang--Mills as the main funnel.  A `FAIL` or `WALL` retires direct
boundary-polymer repair at the Cycle 232 constants; it does not refute
Yarotsky's qualitative theorem or Yang--Mills.

The reason for this asymmetric valuation is exact.  Boundary repair controls
fixed-lattice volumes uniformly at small `lambda`.  The Clay statement also
requires a nontrivial reflection-positive continuum theory and a positive
physical mass gap along a cutoff-removal trajectory.  No estimate in Cycles
230--234 propagates from the certified small-`lambda` interval to
`lambda(a)->infinity`, and no compactness or Osterwalder--Schrader
reconstruction follows from the polymer tilt.

## 3. Main-funnel continuation criterion `CA235`

After `BR235` is terminal, Yang--Mills continues as the Millennium main funnel
if and only if a written continuum-admission packet passes all four tests
below.  The packet may use the `BR235` theorem if it passed, but boundary
success is neither necessary nor sufficient for admission.

1. **Physical scaling.** Specify lattice spacings `a_k -> 0`, bare couplings
   `lambda_k=2/g_k^4 -> infinity`, the rescaling of time and energy, and the
   gauge-invariant observable class.  A fixed-lattice or fixed-small-coupling
   statement fails this test.
2. **Non-equivalent production lemma.** State one new inequality with explicit
   scale-uniform constants that advances either reflection-positive continuum
   tightness/nontriviality or a positive physical mass bound.  It must not
   assume continuum existence, a uniform lattice gap on the same trajectory,
   or the desired Osterwalder--Schrader mass gap under another name.
3. **Finite falsifier.** Freeze a finite block, representation-tail, or
   one-step renormalization test whose exact success and failure outputs are
   specified.  Finite Ritz evidence without an analytic complement bound is
   inadmissible.
4. **Official transfer.** Give a written implication chain from iterating the
   lemma to a named quantified part of the Clay statement, listing the later
   gates that remain.  At least one official quantifier must be advanced before
   simply restating the full problem.

The exact decision rule is therefore:

- `BR235` nonterminal: continue only the frozen Yang--Mills boundary checkpoint;
  do not rotate.
- `BR235` terminal and `CA235 PASS`: continue Yang--Mills, promoting the packet's
  finite falsifier as the next main checkpoint.
- `BR235` terminal and `CA235 FAIL`: archive any boundary theorem or obstruction
  and rotate the main funnel by the six-problem portfolio review.

Difficulty, sunk cost, a qualitative citation, or a numerically attractive
finite block is not a fourth outcome.  Re-entry after rotation requires new
material satisfying `CA235`, not another repair of full-face fusion.

No continuum Yang--Mills theory or mass gap is claimed.
