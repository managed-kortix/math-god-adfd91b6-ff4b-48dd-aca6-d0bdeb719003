# Official statement: Yang--Mills existence and mass gap

Primary source checked 2026-07-25: Arthur Jaffe and Edward Witten,
<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf>.

For every compact simple gauge group `G`, construct a nontrivial quantum
Yang--Mills theory on `R^4` satisfying axioms at least as strong as the cited
Wightman/OS axioms and prove a Hamiltonian spectral gap `Delta>0` above the
vacuum. A fixed lattice, finite volume, one group, gauge-fixed propagator, or
physics heuristic does not meet the quantifiers.
