# Cycle 83: common-cone RG audit

An exact gauge-equivariant and reflection-compatible scale-two blocking exists:
each coarse link is the ordered product of two fine links, and the coarse
measure is the exact pushforward of the Wilson measure. Reflection positivity
is inherited because positive-half observables pull back to positive-half
observables. The induced action, however, contains infinitely many loops,
polymers, representations, and intertwiners.

For `SU(2)`, the exact plaquette character coefficients are

\[
F_j(\beta)={2\over\beta}I_{2j+1}(\beta),
\qquad c_j(\beta)={I_{2j+1}(\beta)\over I_1(\beta)},
\]

with explicit factorial representation-tail bounds. Exact convolution
diagonalizes these coefficients in two dimensions, but in four dimensions link
integration generates the full interaction algebra. A finite two-channel
matrix is only a compression, not a closed RG system.

A fixed positive functional `A+kB` normalized as a distance from the ultraviolet
fixed point is also obstructed perturbatively: asymptotic freedom makes it grow
under infrared coarse-graining. Dropping the UV-minimum interpretation merely
replaces this by unproved pointwise compatibility intervals for `k`.

Most decisively, two channels cannot control the complete vacuum-orthogonal
physical Hilbert space. A valid finite certificate would additionally require:

1. a uniform computable Banach-tail bound for the exact RG map and derivative;
2. constructive entry of the actual weak-coupling continuum trajectory into
   the certified basin at a fixed physical scale;
3. OS tightness, Euclidean restoration, reflection positivity, and a nonzero
   continuum variance;
4. contraction on a cyclic dense set spanning the full physical complement.

These are the central infinite-dimensional and nonperturbative obligations, not
finite bookkeeping. The proposed two-channel common-cone certificate is not
promoted. No Yang--Mills construction or mass-gap result is claimed.
