# Cycle 111: Bring applicability and Hodge rotation gate

Bring's curve rigorously supplies a genus-four curve with smooth canonical
quadric, two trigonal pencils, a Rosati-self-adjoint `Q(sqrt(5))` subfield, and
an integral norm-one automorphism.  It does not satisfy every hypothesis of
Markman's generic genus-four RM secant example.

The decisive issue is
\[
 J(\mathrm{Bring})\sim E^4
\]
for an elliptic curve `E`.  Hence the Jacobian is not simple and its rational
endomorphism algebra is much larger than `Q(sqrt(5))`.  Markman's generic
argument uses simplicity and the RM-only endomorphism hypothesis to identify
the Neron--Severi/invariant algebra and the four-dimensional secant space.  With
Bring's extra endomorphisms, the genericity and nonzero-Weil-projection proof
cannot be imported without a new invariant calculation.

What remains valid is independent of Bring.  For every abelian fourfold
satisfying the stated real-quadratic `(2,2)` eigenspace hypotheses, contraction
by
\[
 N\left(g^*\Theta-\frac q6(g^{-1})^*\Theta^3\right)
\]
has rank `20` and nullity `8`.  The exact verifier survives hostile correction
of the `q` factor, bivector contraction order, and sparse cancellation.  The
kernel decomposes into two trace-free endomorphism modules and two generalized-
deformation lines, but symmetry does not force these representations into the
Atiyah kernel because the Ext target can contain the same types.

No rigorous simple genus-four Jacobian with `End^0=Q(sqrt(5))`, explicit
two-pencil geometry, and instantiated gluing/Ext data was produced.  The HPS
curve remains a heuristic modular candidate; Bring is rigorous but too
endomorphism-rich.  Even on a valid generic RM seed, semiregularity remains an
uncomputed rank-`20` Atiyah assertion.

The quartic-CM route is retired as a production funnel.  The uniform rank-`20`
lemma and verifier are retained as bounded exact work.  No new Hodge case is
claimed.
