## What the strict exterior prefilter would say

This paragraph records a consequence of an additional hypothesis and is not
an actual Atiyah classification.  Suppose the seven-vertex category were
proved formal with only exterior multiplication, and suppose each vertex
class were supplied as a fixed element of `Lambda^2 V` with no transfer
correction.  A degree-one primitive that can hit a diagonal degree-two class
must lie in the reverse block.  Its unshifted Ext degree is `2-e`.  Therefore
the possible diagonal boundary spaces would be

\[
 \mathcal B_q=
 \begin{cases}
 q\wedge V,&e=1,\\
 Kq,&e=2,\\
 0,&3\leq e\leq6.
 \end{cases}
\]

This recovers the Cycle 266 model calculation for `e=2` and `q=a_1a_2`.
It does not evaluate the actual nine twisted columns: their one-vertex
cohomology coordinates are known, but their representatives in a common dg
model and the needed transfer corrections are absent.

## Exact rank-stratum ideals once the missing data are supplied

Fix exterior bases.  Let `D_e(q)` be the exact matrix of

\[
 d_{T_q}:\operatorname {End}^1(T_q)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname {End}^2(T_q)
\]

including all transferred `A_infinity` terms, and let `A_k(q)` be the matrix
whose nine columns are the actual transferred Atiyah cocycles.  Put
`C_k(q)=[D_e(q)\ A_k(q)]`.  The obstruction rank is exactly

\[
 r_k(q)=\operatorname {rank}C_k(q)-\operatorname {rank}D_e(q).
\]

Writing `I_t(M)` for the ideal of `t` by `t` minors, the locally closed stratum
with boundary rank `s` and obstruction rank at most `r` is

\[
 V\bigl(I_{s+1}(D_e)+I_{s+r+1}(C_k)\bigr)
 \setminus V\bigl(I_s(D_e)\bigr).
\]

The all-nine cancellation locus is the union over `s` of

\[
 V\bigl(I_{s+1}(D_e)+I_{s+1}(C_k)\bigr)
 \setminus V\bigl(I_s(D_e)\bigr),
\]

with the irrelevant ideal of the `q` coordinates saturated out.  These are
the required exact Groebner/determinantal ideals.  They cannot be instantiated
without `D_e(q)` and `A_k(q)`; `rho_k` alone supplies neither matrix.
