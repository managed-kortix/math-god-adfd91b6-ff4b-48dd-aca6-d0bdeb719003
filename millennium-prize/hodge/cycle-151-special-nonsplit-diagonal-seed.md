# Cycle 151: an algebraic exceptional seed on a nonsplit Weil sixfold

Let `E_i=C/(Z+iZ)`, put

\[
A_0=E_i^3\times E_i^3,
\]

and let `K=Q(i)` act by the usual CM action on the first triple and the
conjugate action on the second.  Give the factors product polarization weights

\[
(1,1,1;1,1,3).
\]

The resulting polarized abelian sixfold has Weil signature `(3,3)` and
Hermitian matrix, up to the fixed convention,

\[
H=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,-1,-1,-3).
\]

Thus

\[
\det_KH=-3.
\]

This is genuinely nonsplit over `Q(i)`: the inert prime three occurs with odd
valuation in the signed determinant class, whereas norms from `Q_3(i)` have
even valuation.  The polarization type is `(1,1,1,1,1,3)` and its alternating
matrix has determinant nine.  An explicit period is `tau=iI_6`, with mixed
standard/conjugate CM tangent action.

Now take the diagonal graph

\[
\Gamma=\{(x,x):x\in E_i^3\}\subset E_i^3\times E_i^3.
\]

It is an algebraic codimension-three abelian subvariety.  It is not `K`-linear:
the identity between the two blocks is `K`-antilinear.  This extra special-fiber
endomorphism is exactly what allows it to escape the balanced contraction
sector.

With integral one-forms `x_j,y_j` on the first block and primed forms on the
second, its class is

\[
[\Gamma]=\prod_{j=1}^3(x_j-x'_j)\wedge(y_j-y'_j).
\]

For the mixed CM action, the two determinant eigenspaces are generated by

\[
\Omega_W=dz_1dz_2dz_3\,d\bar z_4d\bar z_5d\bar z_6,
\]

\[
\Omega_{\bar W}=d\bar z_1d\bar z_2d\bar z_3\,dz_4dz_5dz_6.
\]

Exact exterior expansion gives nonzero pure coefficients.  Relative to the
integral real basis used by the verifier their oriented coefficients are `+1`
and `-1`.  In the normalized complex one-form convention
`x=(dz+dbar z)/2`, `y=(dz-dbar z)/(2i)`, the rational graph class has the
conjugate pair

\[
\boxed{[\Gamma]_{W^6}=-\frac{i}{8}\Omega_W},
\qquad
\boxed{[\Gamma]_{\bar W^6}=\frac{i}{8}\Omega_{\bar W}}.
\]

Nonvanishing is invariant.  Therefore

\[
\boxed{P_{\rm Weil}[\Gamma]\ne0}.
\]

The rational Weil projector is algebraic, but this requires more than formally
taking an exterior power of a complex eigenspace projector.  Let `u=2+i`.  On
the sector `wedge^p W tensor wedge^(6-p) Wbar`, `u^*` has seven distinct
eigenvalues `(2+i)^p(2-i)^(6-p)`.  Rational interpolation gives a polynomial
`q` equal to one on `p=0,6` and zero on `1,...,5`; thus `q(Gamma_u)` is an
explicit rational algebraic correspondence inducing the Weil projector.

Applying `1+i` acts by conjugate nonreal scalars on the two determinant lines,
so the nonzero projected class and its transform span the rational rank-two
Weil space.  Hence the exceptional Weil classes are algebraic on this explicit
special nonsplit fiber.

This corrects the scope of earlier graph no-go statements.  Generic `K`-linear
graphs and contraction-generated cycles have zero determinant projection, but
an extra `K`-antilinear graph at a decomposable CM point need not.

The result does not settle the very general determinant-`-3` component.  The
diagonal graph deforms only along the proper extra-endomorphism/decomposition
locus.  A dominating relative Chow branch carrying its projected class is not
known.  First-order semiregularity merely places obstruction in a kernel and
does not prove effectivity in all nine Weil-component directions.
