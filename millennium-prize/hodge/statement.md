# Official statement: Hodge conjecture

Primary source checked 2026-07-25: Pierre Deligne,
<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/hodge.pdf>.

For every smooth projective complex variety `X` and every `p`, every class in
`H^(2p)(X,Q) intersect H^(p,p)(X)` is a rational linear combination of cycle
classes of codimension-`p` algebraic subvarieties. Integral, compact-Kähler,
singular, or effective variants are not the official statement.
