## Integral Hermitian divisors

Write the coordinates of `A_0` as `z_1,...,z_6`.  For a Gaussian integer `a`
and `j=1,2,3`, let

\[
D_{a,j}=\{z_{j+3}-az_j=0\}.
\]

This is an integral divisor.  Under
`NS(E_i^6)=Herm_6(Z[i])`, its Hermitian matrix is zero outside indices
`j,j+3` and has block

\[
H_{a,j}=\begin{pmatrix}N(a)&-\bar a\\-a&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

If `a=r+si`, its integral two-form is

\[
\delta_{a,j}=(x_{j+3}-r x_j+s y_j)
\wedge(y_{j+3}-s x_j-r y_j).
\]

transversely with multiplicity one.  Consequently the exact integral identity
is

\[
\Gamma_a=D_{a,1}\cdot D_{a,2}\cdot D_{a,3},
\]

also verified by exterior expansion in the standard integral basis of
`H^6(E_i^6,Z)`.

three integral divisor classes.  Substitution in the Cycle 169 projector gives

\[
