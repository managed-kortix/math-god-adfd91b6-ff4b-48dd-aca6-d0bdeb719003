# Cycle 217: split determinantal supports miss the frozen endpoint degrees

## Frozen support architecture

Work on

\[
 A_0=E_i^3\times E_i^3,
 \qquad P=\operatorname {diag}(1,1,1,1,1,3),
\]

and freeze the Cycle 169 endpoint degrees

\[
 d_+=6072151396206990896,
 \qquad d_-=2315779370123038256.                    \tag{217.1}
\]

The new support ansatz is a standard codimension-three determinantal locus,
not a graph, divisor complete intersection, Ferrand double, liaison residual,
or Pfaffian center.  Freeze

\[
 m_+=161557,
 \qquad m_-=117159,
 \qquad
 E_\epsilon=O(-m_\epsilon P)^{\oplus2},
 \qquad F_\epsilon=O^{\oplus4}.                                  \tag{217.2}
\]

Let

\[
 \Phi_\epsilon:E_\epsilon\longrightarrow F_\epsilon             \tag{217.3}
\]

be a `4 x 2` matrix whose entries are sections of `O(m_epsilon P)`.  The
endpoint equations are

\[
 I(Y^\epsilon)=I_2(\Phi_\epsilon),
 \qquad Y^\epsilon=D_1(\Phi_\epsilon),                            \tag{217.4}
\]

where `I_2` is generated by the six maximal minors.  Restrict the coefficient
space to the open on which (217.4) has pure expected codimension three, is
1-generic, is geometrically integral, and has no embedded component.  These
conditions isolate genuinely determinantal support: its Eagon--Northcott
presentation is not the three-equation complete-intersection architecture
retired in Cycle 197.  All ranks, twists, and support conditions are now fixed.

## Frozen rational-equivalence incidence

Put

\[
 D=d_++d_-=8387930766330029152,
 \qquad Q_0=\operatorname {Chow}_{3,D}(A_0),
\]

and retain the Cycle 169 graph endpoints `C_0^+` and `C_0^-`.  Freeze the
single stratum

\[
 \boxed{R_{(e,n,h)}=R_{(0,1,(1))}}.                              \tag{217.5}
\]

Its variables are `(Phi_+,Phi_-,f)`, subject to the maximal-minor equations
(217.4), the endpoint degree equations

\[
 \deg_PY^+=d_+,
 \qquad \deg_PY^-=d_-,                                           \tag{217.6}
\]

and the evaluation equations

\[
 f:\mathbb P^1\longrightarrow Q_0,
 \qquad \deg(f)=1,
 \qquad
 f(0)=Y^++C_0^-,
 \qquad f(\infty)=Y^-+C_0^+.                                    \tag{217.7}
\]

There is no auxiliary effective cycle because `e=0`.  Equation (217.7) is the
entire rational-equivalence mechanism: if this incidence had a point, the
universal Chow cycle pulled back along `f` would give

\[
 Y^++C_0^-\sim_{\rm rat}Y^-+C_0^+,
 \qquad [Y^+]-[Y^-]=D_0\alpha_0.                                 \tag{217.8}
\]

Thus endpoints, equations, witness complexity, and the proper-support open are
all fixed before taking tangents or quadratic jets.

## Exact degree obstruction

On the expected-codimension open, Thom--Porteous gives

\[
 [Y^\epsilon]
 =c_3(F_\epsilon-E_\epsilon)
 =4m_\epsilon^3P^3.                                             \tag{217.9}
\]

Indeed,

\[
 c_t(F_\epsilon-E_\epsilon)
 =(1-m_\epsilon Pt)^{-2},
 \qquad [t^3](1-m_\epsilon Pt)^{-2}=4m_\epsilon^3P^3.            \tag{217.10}
\]

The polarization has `P^6=2160`, so the Cycle 202 degree convention gives

\[
 \deg_PY^\epsilon={P^3[Y^\epsilon]\over3!}
 =1440m_\epsilon^3.                                             \tag{217.11}
\]

For the frozen twists this is

\[
 \deg_PY^+=6072112820268997920=d_+-38575937992976,
 \qquad
 \deg_PY^-=2315738204136977760=d_--41165986060496.               \tag{217.12}
\]

Therefore neither endpoint degree equation in (217.6) can hold.  The class and
degree are independent of every matrix coefficient, so this contradiction
survives restriction to all the declared support-open conditions.  The residue
check `d_+=296 mod 360` and `d_-=56 mod 360` also shows that no split
maximal-minor locus of this form, at any integral twist, could have either
frozen degree.

There is a second, weaker obstruction: (217.9) puts
`[Y^+]-[Y^-]` in `Z P^3`, whose exceptional Weil projection is zero, whereas
`D_0 alpha_0` has nonzero exceptional projection.  Hence even changing the
endpoint degrees to multiples of `360` would not make this split-polarization
determinantal pair satisfy (217.8).

## Outcome

The frozen incidence `R_(0,1,(1))` has no point with endpoints of the required
degrees, before the rank-nine tangent and second-order equations are reached.
This is an exact architecture obstruction, not a failed parameter search.  It
retires standard expected-codimension-three determinantal loci cut by maximal
minors of maps between sums of polarization powers.  It does not cover
determinantal loci built from nonsplit bundles carrying an independently
constructed exceptional `c_3`; introducing such a bundle would require a new,
noncircular construction.  No Hodge-conjecture result is claimed.
