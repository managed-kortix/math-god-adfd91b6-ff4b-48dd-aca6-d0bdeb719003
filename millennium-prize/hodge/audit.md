# Hostile audit

## Cycle 287 mixed-product guard

Do not confuse an abelian variety whose simple isogeny factors have dimension
at most four with an abelian variety of total dimension at most four.
Moonen--Zarhin's low-dimensional theorem does not apply to an arbitrary
product of Kummer intermediate Jacobians. Likewise,
`forall A forall r HC(A^r)` is not the same quantifier as Hodge for mixed
products of different `A`; common semisimple or toric Hodge-group quotients can
create cross tensors. A common Weil field, pairwise nonisogeny, or separate
algebraicity of each factor's Weil classes does not by itself remove them.

The recorded full-`SU` subcase uses every hypothesis. Consolidate isogeny
classes first; require the full expected special unitary Hodge group in the
natural rational representation, not just a Weil action or discriminant one;
use the standard-plus-dual representation to turn any `A_3` graph coupling
into a weight-one Hodge morphism; and use discriminant one for the
all-self-powers Hodge input.  Its generalized-Kummer transfer additionally
uses Floccari--Fu `arXiv:2610.31740v1`, Theorem 1.4, conditional on Lefschetz
standard, composed literally with Foster `arXiv:2512.04114v2`, Theorem
1.7/8.15, which proves that condition in complex dimension `4d`.  The
homological-motive conclusion must be used as algebraic split summands before
Hodge classes are mapped out and back.  Repeated isogeny classes are common
motivic generators, not independent factors. Do not extend this proof to
special points with extra endomorphisms, CM tori, decompositions, or smaller
Hodge groups, except for the separately precise case in which all Jacobians
are isogenous to one common generator. See
`../cycle-287-kummer-mixed-product-classification.md`.

## Cycle 286 boundary guard

Cycle 284 remains valid exactly as stated, but its formal closure is narrow.
For distinct covered `K_i`, self-power Hodge for each `K_i` does not imply
Hodge for a mixed product: cross-Hodge tensors live in the joint tensor
category generated by the different intermediate Jacobians. A common
algebraic motivic generator with Hodge on all its powers is sufficient; merely
having separate generators is not. Disjoint unions and direct sums are
circular because their powers already contain the mixed products. Passing to
Andre motivated correspondences does not prove algebraicity of the missing
cross-correspondences.

Do not append an arbitrary abelian factor: a theorem for `K x A` for every
abelian `A` would imply Hodge for every `A` by projection and refined pullback.
Do not extend the composition to dimension `4d+2`: Foster's odd-`n` range stops
strictly below degree `n+1`, while Floccari--Fu's full-motive theorem itself
uses even-`n` canonical-cycle input. The separately known six-dimensional
`Kum^3` base theorem is not an all-self-powers statement unless a source is
cited for that stronger quantifier. See
`../cycle-286-generalized-kummer-boundary-audit.md`.

## Cycle 284 external-corollary guard

The composition of Foster `arXiv:2512.04114v2`, Theorem 8.15, with
Floccari--Fu `arXiv:2610.31740v1`, Theorem 1.4, is accepted only as a
version-pinned external corollary. It covers rational Hodge classes on
self-powers `K^r` where one projective generalized-Kummer-type base `K` has
complex dimension `4d`. Do not silently enlarge "all powers" to mixed products
of distinct varieties, generalized-Kummer dimensions `4d+2`, other
hyper-Kähler types, or arbitrary smooth projective varieties. Do not cite the
five-days-earlier Floccari--Fu Remark 1.5 as a surviving condition without
checking Foster v2, and do not describe direct theorem composition as a
project theorem. Any source revision requires renewed hypothesis audit.

Do not confuse rational with integral Hodge, algebraicity of the Hodge locus
with algebraicity of its class, or tangent-space equality with global
dominance. Audit monodromy, nonreduced Hilbert components, specialization, and
whether the cycle has exactly the prescribed primitive class.
