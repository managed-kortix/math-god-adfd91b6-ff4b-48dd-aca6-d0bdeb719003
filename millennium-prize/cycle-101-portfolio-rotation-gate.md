# Cycle 101: structural portfolio gate

## BSD: genuine higher-rank systems

Burns--Sano's exterior-bidual and determinant formalism genuinely constructs
rank-`r` Euler and Kolyvagin systems.  For `T_pE`, the canonical archimedean core
rank is one over `Q` and two over an imaginary quadratic field.  Thus a genuine
rank-two system naturally lives over such a field; the `389a1` Kurihara
certificate over `Q` cannot be upgraded by terminology.

Vertical determinant systems are unconditionally rank-one modules and map to
basic Euler systems.  Under the standard cohomological hypotheses, rank-two
derivatives control higher Fitting ideals.  However the Heegner rank-two element
is integral/extendable to a complete Euler system only with main-conjecture or
BSD-level input in the currently relevant constructions.  An unconditional
new checkpoint would require explicit residual determinant classes, vanishing
of the appropriate `H^0,H^2`, and a determinant lift over an imaginary
quadratic tower without assuming the Heegner-point main conjecture.  No such
new instance was produced.

## Yang--Mills: physical-slab contraction

Let `T_(a,L)` be the vacuum-normalized positive transfer operator on the full
physical Hilbert space, `P` its vacuum projection, and `Q=I-P`.  If for one
physical thickness `r_0` and one `q<1`, uniformly in cutoff and volume,
\[
 Q T_{a,L}^{\lceil r_0/a\rceil}Q\le qQ,
\]
then any nontrivial OS continuum limit with convergence on a dense physical
observable algebra has mass gap at least
\[
 \log(1/q)/r_0.
\]
This exact norm inequality detects cutoff-escaping eigenvectors and is stronger
than every fixed-channel compression.  Reflection positivity gives only the
nonstrict bound `q=1`; proving strict uniform contraction on the entire vacuum
complement is the missing nonperturbative estimate.

## Navier--Stokes: helical phase residence

For an isolated helical triad with amplitudes `a_j=r_j exp(i phi_j)`, the total
interaction phase satisfies
\[
 \dot\Theta=-\sin\Theta\sum_j C_j\frac{r_1r_2r_3}{r_j^2}.
\]
The manifolds `Theta=0,pi` are exactly invariant, including under diagonal
Navier damping.  Choosing the locked sign gives positive vortex stretching for
as long as amplitudes remain nonzero; explicit Euler separatrices retain one
stretching sign for an entire half-line.  Therefore no universal sign-only
phase-residence bound exists.  Only magnitude-thresholded, data-dependent
damping estimates survive.

## RH: random Euler products

Random multiplicative functions have almost-sure square-root cancellation and
zero-free random Euler products in `Re(s)>1/2`.  Möbius is a probability-zero,
maximally exceptional prime-sign configuration.  In biased models the exact
factorization
\[
 F_\alpha(s)=\Theta_\alpha(s)/\zeta(s+\alpha)
\]
shows that deterministic zeta dependence is inserted through the mean rather
than derived by concentration.  No support-to-point rigidity transfers typical
random behavior to the actual primes.

## Hodge: abelian-type motives

Motivated equals algebraic on a tensor category generated by abelian varieties
only after the all-products Hodge conjecture for the generators is known.  A
safe invariant-theoretic subclass occurs when the joint motivated group equals
the expected Lefschetz group and every invariant is generated by polarization
contractions and algebraic endomorphisms.  Exceptional tensors such as Weil
classes mark the boundary.  Extending this closure to new mixed abelian Chow
summands could yield bounded Hodge cases, but no novelty-verified new class was
established here.

## P versus NP: algebraic proof compression

Superpolynomial IPS lower bounds imply `VP!=VNP`, not Boolean `P!=NP`.  A modest
Boolean magnification would need an independent junk-robust theorem compressing
every polynomial-size proof under `P=NP` to near-linear size.  No such geometric
normal-form or syzygy-compression theorem is known; without it, a fixed
`n^(1+epsilon)` lower bound does not contradict an unspecified polynomial upper
bound.

All six routes met exact gates.  No Millennium result is claimed.
