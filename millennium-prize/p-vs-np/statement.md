# Official statement: P versus NP

Primary source checked 2026-07-25: Stephen Cook,
<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pvsnp.pdf>.

Determine whether every language accepted nondeterministically in polynomial
time is accepted deterministically in polynomial time in the standard uniform
Turing-machine model. Restricted models, oracle separations, finite lengths,
average-case behavior, or nonuniform quantifier mistakes do not settle it.
