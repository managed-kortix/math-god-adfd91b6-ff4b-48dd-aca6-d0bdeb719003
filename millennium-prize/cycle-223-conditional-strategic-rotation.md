## Selected structural gate

Rotate the main funnel to focused portfolio discovery through the
**Navier coupled inverse-transfer cascade admission gate**.  This is one
structural re-entry test, not a survey of PDE routes.  Before any numerical
campaign, require one written architecture with all of the following data.

1. A deterministic depth-`J` family of real, divergence-free Fourier packets,
   including exact frequencies, polarizations, phases, amplitudes, and the
   ordering of transfers.  Frequency separation or disconnected replication
   alone is inadmissible by the Cycle 213 no-go.
2. An exact signed interaction lemma showing sequential transfer toward lower
   velocity frequencies while bounding reverse transfer, off-cascade outputs,
   and packet collisions by one declared positive budget, uniformly in `J`.
3. A quantitative endpoint inequality whose lower bound exceeds two by an
   explicit margin `eta>0` for one finite declared depth.  Scaling, packet
   count, or coarse-grid aliasing may not be the source of the margin.
4. A residual estimate in a norm accepted by the Cycle 215 full-PDE enclosure
   interface, together with an explicit inequality showing that the margin
   survives viscosity, Fourier tails, time discretization, and directed
   endpoint `L^3` cubature.

The admission gate passes only when these four items are proved on paper and
freeze one finite candidate for exact enclosure; only then does Navier--Stokes
become the assigned proof lane.  It fails when an exact identity, budget
inequality, or scaling obstruction rules out the declared cascade architecture.
Failure keeps the program in portfolio discovery; it does not authorize random
starts, larger Fourier boxes, or a weaker amplification target.

No Navier--Stokes, Hodge, or other Millennium result is claimed.
