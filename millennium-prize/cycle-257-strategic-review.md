## Exact decision

Record:

`CYCLE255 FROZEN NUMERICAL FUNNEL RETIRED FROM PRODUCTION: 0/10038 PROMOTIONS AT 1.5; NUMERICAL ONLY.`

Retire only the frozen packet/tail/time family from further production runs
under its declared thresholds. This is an operational stop decision, not an
exact family exclusion. Do not increase resolution, lower the promotion
threshold, tune the 781 packet profiles, alter the tail amplitudes, or send the
numerical maximizer to interval certification merely to continue this family.

Keep `ND251` as the main funnel, but return it to production-architecture
discovery. A new architecture must specify a genuinely different deterministic
smooth Euler mechanism and a finite numerical promotion rule before compute.
It may reuse the exact Cycle 255 shrinking-analytic-tail, full-PDE enclosure,
endpoint-cubature, and Cycle 256 transfer infrastructure. Reusing those proof
interfaces does not authorize reusing or locally tuning the retired family.

The new architecture earns no finite-falsifiability score until it is frozen,
and numerical promotion would still be candidate selection only. The required
claim remains one rigorous smooth Euler orbit satisfying `ND251`, followed by
the exact full-PDE transfer packet. No Navier--Stokes or Millennium result is
claimed.
