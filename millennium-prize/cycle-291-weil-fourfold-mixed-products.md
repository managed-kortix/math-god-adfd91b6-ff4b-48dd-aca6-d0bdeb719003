# Cycle 291: conditional mixed-product closure and the unresolved derived-support wall

## Scope and binary status

Let
\[
 B_1,\ldots,B_r
\]
be complex abelian fourfolds of Weil type and discriminant one.  The target is
whether the rational Hodge conjecture holds for every finite mixed product
\[
 \prod_{i=1}^r B_i^{m_i},\qquad m_i\geq 0.
\]

**Current gate: NO unconditional theorem.**  Floccari--Fu's all-self-powers
theorem, the low-dimensional results of Moonen--Zarhin, and the Hodge
conjecture for CM abelian varieties do not by themselves prove the required
finite-family derived independence.  The local determinant quotient bridge
and the aggregate fiber-product lemma have been independently reconstructed
and hostilely audited below; the proof now stops exactly at an exhaustive
rational support-and-packet census and the graph-rigidity lemmas needed to
complete it.  The first two unresolved graph cases are:

1. a graph between two simple type-III outer-`D_2` packets, where one must
   identify the actual rational eight-dimensional `H^1` modules, their Tits
   data, and the parametrized projected Hodge circles; and
2. a graph between two outer-`A_2` packets, where one must descend the
   standard/dual matching and then recover the uniquely lifted, parametrized
   Hodge generator.

The first of these is the earliest open graph case.  The census must also rule
out every partial packet coincidence involving repeated lower-dimensional
supports; matrix embeddings of the Weil field into an isotypic endomorphism
algebra cannot be ignored.  Consequently the equality
\[
 \operatorname{Hg}\!\left(C\times\prod_\alpha X_\alpha\right)^{\mathrm{der}}
 =\prod_\alpha\operatorname{Hg}(X_\alpha)^{\mathrm{der}}
 \tag{DI}
\]
is an **assumption**, not Proposition 291.2.  Every joint conclusion below
marked `[DI+QB]` depends on literal equality (DI) in the given block-diagonal
representation, not merely on equality of Lie algebras or on an isogeny.

This record is deliberately **fail-closed**.  It makes no positive
mixed-product assertion unless both of the following gates are imposed
simultaneously.  The tag `[DI+QB]` below always means conditional on both gates,
not on either one separately.

### Gate DI: literal embedded derived equality

After an exhaustive support census and consolidation of repeated isogeny
supports, the equality
\[
 H^{\mathrm{der}}=\prod_\alpha D_\alpha
\]
must hold as an equality of closed rational subgroup schemes in the actual
block-diagonal representation.  Equality of Lie algebras, equality after an
isogeny, or equality only on adjoint groups does not satisfy DI.

### Gate QB: algebraic faithful exact quotient bridges

For every exact quotient
\[
 A_\alpha=G_\alpha/D_\alpha,
\]
one must specify a faithful rational representation
`rho_alpha:A_alpha -> GL(W_alpha)`, an object in the tensor category generated
by `h(X_alpha)` on which `G_alpha` acts through exactly
`rho_alpha circ (G_alpha -> A_alpha)`, an object in the category generated by
`h(C)`, and an **algebraic isomorphism in rational homological motives**
between those two objects.  Compatibility is required with the actual
quotient map, not with a power of it or with an isogenous torus.  In
particular, the bridge must retain the finite central intersection
\[
 Z(G_\alpha)\cap D_\alpha
\]
and all finite-isogeny data.  Trivial quotients require no bridge.

Section 4 gives an explicit construction of QB in the only presently
identified nontrivial row.  It uses the entire rational Weil plane and a
normalized algebraic image projector, not an unspecified component of one
class.  That local construction does not use DI, but the assertion that it is
exhaustive still depends on the support census.  It is not converted into any
joint categorical conclusion unless **both** DI and QB are available for the
complete inventory.  Section 5 records only the resulting formal implication
`[DI+QB]`.  This is a wall dossier, not a theorem, a solved-problem claim, or a
publication trigger.

## 1. Source inputs and reduction to supports

The source inputs currently available are the following.

1. **Floccari--Fu, Theorem 1.2.**  The rational Hodge conjecture holds for all
   powers of each individual abelian fourfold of Weil type and discriminant
   one.
2. **Moonen--Zarhin.**  Their low-dimensional analysis gives the individual
   simple factors and Hodge groups needed in the cases it treats.  Section
   5.3 gives the determinant/norm relation in the exceptional `3+1` case.
   Section 2.5(ii) is used only for its stated narrowing of a simple balanced
   fourfold to type III, IV(1,1), or IV(4,1); it is not a pairwise rational
   graph-rigidity theorem.
3. **van Hoften--Sempliner, Theorem I.**  The rational Hodge conjecture holds
   for every complex CM abelian variety, hence for all powers of the aggregate
   CM support used below.

Up to isogeny, consolidate repeated simple factors and write
\[
 \prod_i B_i\sim C\times\prod_{\alpha=1}^sX_\alpha^{n_\alpha},
 \tag{291.1}
\]
where `C` is the product of all CM simple supports and the `X_alpha` are
pairwise nonisogenous simple non-CM supports.  Put
\[
 G_\alpha=\operatorname{Hg}(X_\alpha),\qquad
 D_\alpha=G_\alpha^{\mathrm{der}},\qquad
 S=\operatorname{Hg}(C),
\]
and
\[
 H=\operatorname{Hg}\!\left(C\times\prod_\alpha X_\alpha\right).
\]
If `C=0`, interpret `S` as the trivial group.  Otherwise `S` is the
**actual joint CM Hodge torus**; it must not be replaced by a product of the
Hodge tori of the individual CM factors.

The individual fullness assertion used later really does descend to a simple
support.  If `X_alpha` occurs in `B_i`, the isogeny decomposition of `B_i`
gives algebraic homological correspondences exhibiting `h(X_alpha)` as a
direct summand of `h(B_i)`.  Floccari--Fu gives the Hodge conjecture for every
power of that same `B_i`, equivalently fullness of Betti realization on the
pseudo-abelian tensor category generated by `h(B_i)`.  Fullness therefore
restricts to the tensor category generated by the summand `h(X_alpha)`, and to
any collection of supports lying in that same original fourfold.  This does
**not** imply fullness for the category jointly generated by supports coming
from different fourfolds; that is exactly the mixed-product problem.

## 3. The unproved derived-support proposition

Every projection `H -> G_alpha` is surjective by minimality of the Hodge
group, and hence every projection
\[
 H^{\mathrm{der}}\longrightarrow D_\alpha
\]
is surjective.  The next step must use a finite-family Goursat statement at
the level of **rational simple ideals or adjoint packets**.  It is incorrect
to say without qualification that every `D_alpha` is rationally
almost-simple: for example, `Res_(F/Q) SL_2` is one rational packet although it
splits geometrically, and outer `D_2` also has two geometric vertices that
need not descend separately.

A proper semisimple subdirect product would yield a rational graph between
isomorphic adjoint packets in distinct coordinates.  Because this graph comes
from the actual joint Hodge group, it carries the **parametrized projected
Hodge circle** in one quotient to the parametrized projected Hodge circle in
the other.  Equality only of their images or of their real conjugacy classes
is not enough.  To contradict consolidation, one must then prove that the
fixed rational graph produces a nonzero rational weight-one Hodge map between
the relevant `H^1` modules; a Hodge map between simple abelian supports would
produce an isogeny.

That final implication is not currently proved in the following two cases.

### 3.1 First wall: type-III `D_2/D_2`

Over an algebraic closure, both `H^1` modules have the form
`2(2 box-times 2)`, and the diagram involution preserves this absolute weight.
This does not identify the rational representations.  For a fixed rational
adjoint isomorphism
\[
 \varphi:G_A^{\mathrm{ad}}\xrightarrow{\sim}G_B^{\mathrm{ad}},
\]
one still has to prove that pulling back the `H^1(B)` representation identifies
its vector-weight Tits algebra, including the effect of vertex exchange and
the correct opposite-algebra convention, with that of `H^1(A)`.  Equality of
absolute highest weights cannot supply this descent datum.

An adequate repair must at least establish all of the following for arbitrary
special simple type-III fourfolds in the locus:

1. the relevant centralizer identity relating
   `End_Hg(H^1)` to `End^0(A)` and its center-rational quaternion algebra;
2. invariance of the rational eight-dimensional module/Tits class under every
   allowed rational graph, including the diagram involution; and
3. the exact equality
   `varphi_R(J_A)=J_B` for the parametrized projected generators, after which
   a rational intertwiner is a weight-one Hodge map.

Moonen--Zarhin's individual Hodge-group result and Abdulali's description of
the type-III PEL/general-member representation do not, as presently cited,
state this two-object rigidity lemma for every special member.  Thus the line
"the rational Tits descent is unique" must be deleted unless a proof or an
exact source is supplied.

### 3.2 Second wall: outer `A_2/A_2`

On a chosen complex standard constituent, the semisimple part of the complex
structure has eigenvalues
\[
 2i/3,\ -4i/3
\]
with multiplicities `2,1`, up to simultaneous sign, and there is a unique
scalar correction giving full eigenvalues in `{+i,-i}`.  This calculation is
useful only **after** one has descended a standard-versus-dual matching.

For an arbitrary rational isomorphism between the outer adjoint `A_2` forms,
one must still prove that the matching descends through the quadratic Galois
action and the relevant Tits-algebra data, and that the graph relation gives
the exact parametrized lifted generator to which the scalar-correction
calculation applies.  Moonen--Zarhin section 2.3 determines the individual
unitary Hodge group; it is not this pairwise graph-rigidity theorem.

Accordingly, the old proof of (DI) is retracted.  The remaining graph table
must also be written packet-by-packet, including the type-II(2) and IV(2,1)
checks above, before (DI) can be restored.

## 4. Audit of the only candidate nontrivial quotient bridge

This section constructs the exact QB data for the outer-`A_2` threefold row.
Its local calculations are independent of the failed graph classification.
They do **not** imply a mixed-product statement; every use of this bridge in
the joint category remains `[DI+QB]`, and exhaustiveness of the row still
belongs to the unresolved support census.

Let `T` be a non-CM simple threefold support with
`End^0(T)=K`, where `K` is imaginary quadratic.  Dimension forces it to occur
inside one original fourfold as
\[
 B\sim E_K\times T. \tag{291.2}
\]
Because the factors are nonisogenous, the unital map from the original Weil
field to `End^0(B)=End^0(E_K) x End^0(T)` projects unitaly to both factors.
Thus `E_K` has CM by the same field (up to conjugate identification), and is
already one of the CM supports included in `C`; no auxiliary elliptic curve is
being adjoined.

Write `U_K=ker(Res_(K/Q) G_m -> G_m)` and let `(V,psi)` be the hermitian
`K`-space attached to `T`.  There is an exact sequence of connected rational
algebraic groups
\[
 1\longrightarrow SU_K(V,\psi)
 \longrightarrow U_K(V,\psi)
 \xrightarrow{\det_K}U_K
 \longrightarrow1. \tag{291.3}
\]
After base change to an algebraic closure this is
\[
 1\longrightarrow SL_3\longrightarrow GL_3
 \xrightarrow{\det}G_m\longrightarrow1.
\]
Consequently `det_K` is the primitive determinant character and has connected
kernel.  The underlying rational two-dimensional multiplication
representation of `U_K` on `K` is faithful, so it detects the actual quotient,
not merely an isogenous torus.  The restriction to the scalar center is
`z -> z^3`, but this is no defect: its kernel is precisely
`Z(U_K(V,psi)) cap SU_K(V,psi)`, the rational three-torsion group scheme whose
geometric fiber is `mu_3`.  This is exactly the finite central overlap already
present in (291.3).

Put
\[
 A=H^1(E_K,\mathbb Q),\qquad
 L_T=\left(\bigwedge_K^3H^1(T,\mathbb Q)\right)(1).
 \tag{291.4}
\]
Both are rational rank-two weight-one Hodge structures, or equivalently
one-dimensional `K`-Hodge structures after an orientation is chosen.
Moonen--Zarhin section 1.9 gives the exact factorization
\[
 W_K(E_K\times T)=W_K(E_K)\otimes_KW_K(T)
 =H^1(E_K)\otimes_K\bigwedge_K^3H^1(T),
\]
where the harmless use of homology or dual cohomology is converted by the
polarizations; the displayed Tate twist below fixes the weight convention.
There are no omitted exterior summands: this is the whole rational
2-dimensional Weil plane in the mixed Kunneth component.  Section 5.3 further
proves, up to conjugate orientation,
\[
 \operatorname{Hg}(E_K\times T)
 =\{(u,g)\in U_K\times U_K(V,\psi):u\det_K(g)=1\}, \tag{291.5}
\]
and identifies that Weil plane with the complete Hodge-Hom packet.  Thus every
nonzero rational `c` in the plane gives a nonzero Hodge map
\[
 f_c:A\longrightarrow L_T. \tag{291.6}
\]
Since source and target are one-dimensional over `K`, this map is an
isomorphism.  For the natural `K`-actions it is `K`-antilinear; equivalently it
is `K`-linear after conjugating one action.  Reversing the chosen embedding
replaces the `U_K` coordinate by its inverse.  The untwisted dual formulation
is
\[
 \bigwedge_K^3H^1(T)\simeq H^1(E_K)^\vee(-2)
 \simeq H^1(E_K)(-1),
\]
with no half-Tate twist.

The bridge (291.6) is algebraic in rational homological abelian motives, and
its image projector can be constructed without assuming beforehand that a raw
motivic `K`-exterior power exists:

1. Every vector of `W_K(B)` is a rational Hodge class on the same fourfold
   `B`.  Floccari--Fu therefore makes a chosen nonzero `c` algebraic.  The
   algebraic Kunneth projectors, the algebraic `K`-endomorphisms, and
   polarization identify its mixed component with an algebraic homological
   correspondence inducing `f_c`.
2. Let `f_c^dagger` be its adjoint for algebraic polarizations.  Positivity and
   injectivity make `f_c^dagger f_c` invertible in `End^0(E_K)`.  Its inverse
   is again an algebraic rational endomorphism.  Hence
   \[
    p_T=f_c(f_c^\dagger f_c)^{-1}f_c^\dagger
   \]
   is an algebraic idempotent on `h^3(T)(1)` whose Betti image is exactly
   `L_T`.  Define `Delta_T` to be this image.
3. The map `f_c:h^1(E_K)->Delta_T` and
   `(f_c^dagger f_c)^{-1}f_c^dagger` are mutually inverse in rational
   homological motives.  Thus no unproved algebraic projector and no
   unspecified projection of a Weil class is being used.

This gives the precise homological-motive isomorphism required by QB for this
row.  Two independent hostile reconstructions found no defect in the full
rational packet, normalized projector, primitive determinant, faithful
rational quotient representation, or finite `mu_3` overlap.  It is not a
Chow-motive assertion: a Chow-motive inverse would require a separate
nilpotence/lifting argument.

The finite data are part of the check, not an optional refinement.  The
faithful rational representation of `U_K` is pulled back along the primitive
map `det_K`, and its restriction to the scalar center records the kernel
`mu_3` in (291.3).  Replacing `det_K` by an isogenous character, or retaining
only its differential, would not discharge QB.

For every such `T_alpha`, this construction gives an exact quotient bridge to
the actual CM elliptic submotive already in `C`.  Its Hodge action defines the
actual morphism
\[
 q_\alpha:S=\operatorname{Hg}(C)\longrightarrow U_{K_\alpha},
\]
with inversion when the conjugate orientation requires it.  All bridges live
simultaneously in the one category generated by `C`; no auxiliary pairwise CM
torus is introduced.  The use of these maps in the joint fiber product is
**only** the conditional step `[DI+QB]`.  The aggregate `S`, rather than a
presumed product of separate CM tori, records all simultaneous quotient
relations.  Fullness for the category generated by that single aggregate CM
object algebraizes its multiway CM tensors, but this fact alone does not
establish DI and does not yield the mixed-product claim.

## 5. Formal categorical implication `[DI+QB]`

The next lemma is a conditional group-theoretic/Tannakian statement.  It is
not an assertion that DI or QB holds in the present family.

### Lemma 291.C (exact quotient pullback under both gates)

Let `M_1,...,M_s,C` be rational homological motives generated by abelian
varieties.  Let `G_alpha` and `S` be their polarization-fixed Hodge groups,
let `D_alpha=G_alpha^der`, and put `A_alpha=G_alpha/D_alpha`.  Assume:

1. Betti realization is full on each category generated by one `M_alpha` and
   on the category generated by `C`;
2. **DI:** the derived group of the joint Hodge group is the literal embedded
   product `K=product D_alpha`;
3. **QB:** for every nontrivial `A_alpha`, a faithful rational quotient
   representation with the actual map `G_alpha -> A_alpha` is algebraically
   identified with an object generated by `C`, including all finite central
   overlap and finite-isogeny data.

Then, and only as the formal implication `[DI+QB]`, Betti realization is full
on the category jointly generated by `M_1,...,M_s,C`.

#### Proof of the conditional implication

Let `P` be the tensor automorphism group defined by algebraic homological
correspondences in the joint category, and let `H` be its Hodge group.  Since
algebraic correspondences are Hodge,
\[
 H\subseteq P.
\]
Individual fullness and generation of the joint category give a closed
embedding
\[
 P\hookrightarrow S\times\prod_\alpha G_\alpha.
\]

For each nontrivial `A_alpha`, QB supplies an actual morphism
`q_alpha:S -> A_alpha`.  Because the quotient representation is faithful and
the bridge is algebraic, every element of `P` preserves its graph.  Thus, as
an equality of subgroup schemes rather than only identity-component Lie
algebras,
\[
 P\subseteq F:=
 \left\{(s,(g_\alpha)):
   \pi_\alpha(g_\alpha)=q_\alpha(s)
   \text{ in }A_\alpha\text{ for every }\alpha
 \right\},                                      \tag{291.7}
\]
where `pi_alpha:G_alpha -> A_alpha` is the exact quotient and the condition is
vacuous when `A_alpha=1`.  Faithfulness is what prevents (291.7) from silently
replacing `pi_alpha` by an isogenous quotient and thereby losing a finite
central kernel.

The quotient maps `pi_alpha` are faithfully flat with kernels `D_alpha`, so
projection to the first coordinate gives the exact fppf sequence
\[
 1\longrightarrow K:=\prod_\alpha D_\alpha
 \longrightarrow F\longrightarrow S\longrightarrow1. \tag{291.8}
\]
This remains exact when `S` imposes genuinely multiway relations among the
quotient coordinates: those relations alter the image in
`product A_alpha`, not the independently liftable kernels.

The projection `H -> S` is faithfully flat because `S` is the Hodge group of
the corresponding factor.  The bridge tensors give `H subseteq F`.  DI gives
the literal subgroup
\[
 K=\prod_\alpha D_\alpha\subseteq H.
\]
These two facts imply `H=F`: fppf-locally, choose for a point of `F` a point of
`H` with the same `S`-coordinate; their quotient lies in `K`, hence in `H`.
Thus the argument proves equality of group schemes and retains finite central
data.  Therefore
\[
 H\subseteq P\subseteq F=H,
\]
and `P=H`.  Tannakian duality, in the polarization-fixed category with Tate
objects normalized, gives fullness of Betti realization.  This proves only
the displayed conditional implication.  `QED`

For the supports of (291.1), the retract argument in section 1 supplies the
individual-fullness hypothesis.  Section 4 certifies the exact algebraic
quotient bridge for every `3+1` row once that row has been identified by the
support census; semisimple rows need no bridge.  Thus the only unclosed
mathematical gate is DI together with the exhaustiveness of the support census
that defines its coordinates.  In particular, the certified quotient bridge
cannot repair a missing semisimple factor.

## 6. Fail-closed boundaries

* Separate all-self-powers theorems do not imply the mixed-product statement.
* The former Proposition 291.2 and Theorem 291.1 are retracted, not merely
  missing citations.
* Complex highest-weight matching does not prove rational Tits-module descent.
* Equality of real conjugacy classes of Hodge circles does not give equality
  of parametrized circles under a fixed rational graph.
* Equality up to isogeny is insufficient at either gate.  DI requires the
  literal embedded derived subgroup; QB requires a faithful representation of
  the actual quotient map and preservation of every finite central overlap.
* The local determinant calculation, even if it completely discharges QB,
  cannot repair failure of DI.
* The motivic bridges discussed here belong only to rational homological
  abelian motives, not to Chow motives.
* No theorem, novelty claim, Clay claim, or publication trigger is recorded.
