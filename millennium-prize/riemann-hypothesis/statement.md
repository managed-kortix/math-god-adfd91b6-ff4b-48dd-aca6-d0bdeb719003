# Official statement: Riemann hypothesis

Primary source checked 2026-07-25: Enrico Bombieri, official Clay problem
description, <https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/riemann.pdf>.

For the meromorphic continuation of the Riemann zeta function,

\[
 \forall\rho\in\mathbb C,\quad
 [\zeta(\rho)=0\ \wedge\ 0<\Re\rho<1]
 \Longrightarrow \Re\rho=\tfrac12.
\]

A proof must cover every nontrivial zero. A disproof requires a rigorously
isolated zero off the critical line. Finite-height verification, an
uncertified floating-point zero, or an equivalent criterion without proving
it is not a solution.
