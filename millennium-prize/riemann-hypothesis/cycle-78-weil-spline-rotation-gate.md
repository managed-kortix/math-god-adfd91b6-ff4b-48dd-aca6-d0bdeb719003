# Cycle 78: Weil-spline rotation gate

For nested finite spline spaces, positivity of every generalized Schur
innovation is exactly positivity of every finite Weil matrix. In the singular
case one must include

\[
\operatorname{ran}B\subseteq\operatorname{ran}A,
\qquad C-B^*A^\dagger B\succeq0.
\]

With continuity of the Weil form on the compactly supported `H^1` completion
and density of rational splines, positivity at every level is equivalent to the
full Weil criterion. It is therefore bookkeeping, not an intermediate sign
theorem.

Termwise positivity is false. Prime-power translation packets are indefinite,
the pole packet is low rank and can be indefinite under the real bilinear
coordinates, and the archimedean matrix need not be positive. A support-uniform
domination of all positive prime translations by the logarithmic gamma form and
fixed-rank pole moments is obstructed by long packets: the prime mass grows
exponentially with support while gamma energy remains bounded.

The mesh-`1/2`, support-`[-1,1]` three-hat matrix is positive definite in a
high-precision calibration, with approximate eigenvalues

\[
0.0001777525749,\quad0.0008275302911,\quad0.001179681388.
\]

This depends on cancellation among much larger indefinite channels and is only
finite evidence. The Weil-spline refinement tactic is retired. No RH result is
claimed.
