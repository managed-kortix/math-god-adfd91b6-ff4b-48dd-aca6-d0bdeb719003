# Routes

Cycle 210 promotes the universal factor-two amplification bound for the
mean-zero `L^3` norm of every maximal smooth periodic solution. Together with
endpoint `L^infinity_t L^3_x` continuation, it would imply the periodic
existence alternative. The exact first gate is an exhaustive, interval-
validated campaign on a frozen five-frequency family of embedded 2D data,
seven effective viscosities, and rational checkpoint times. One full-PDE
certificate with final amplification strictly greater than two refutes the
universal lemma; a Galerkin trajectory or floating ratio does not. Exhaustion
without a crossing retires only that finite family. See
`../cycle-210-strategic-rotation.md`.
