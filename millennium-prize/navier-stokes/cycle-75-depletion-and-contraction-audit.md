# Cycle 75: depletion and Duhamel-contraction audit

Exact opposing Fourier waves generating one fixed low output show that the
second Picard contribution scales quadratically with amplitude. Hence a
lifespan-independent high--high contraction is false with a perturbative data
remainder, while an unrestricted quadratic remainder can absorb the whole
interaction and makes the contraction vacuous.

Even if assumed, high--high contraction leaves

\[
(1-\theta)X\le A(u_0)+C\nu^{-1}X^2,
\qquad X=\|u\|_{L^\infty_tB^0_{3,2}},
\]

which permits a large branch. Time subdivision does not help: arbitrarily high
dyadic heat kernels have full `L^1` mass on every positive interval.

Energy dissipation gives the valid estimate

\[
\|H_j\|_{L^\infty_IL^3}
\lesssim {2^j\over\nu}\sum_{k\ge j-C}2^{-2(k-j)}z_kz_k^*,
\qquad z_k^2=\nu\int_I2^{2k}\|u_k\|_2^2dt.
\]

It repairs the infrared shell sum but retains an ultraviolet cutoff factor.

Direction coherence, sparseness, and isotropic zero-mode quadrupole also do not
force depletion. Three equal orthogonal packet families have zero orientation
quadrupole, while changing one sideband phase preserves it and produces a
nonzero projected fixed low mode with no carrier-frequency decay. Backscatter
is phase-sensitive; the quadrupole is only a zero-lag moment.

Both proposed depletion mechanisms are retired. No regularity or blowup result
is claimed.
