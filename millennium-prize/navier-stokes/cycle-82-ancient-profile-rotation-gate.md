# Cycle 82: terminal ancient-profile rotation gate

Blowup rescaling preserves the global critical norm:

\[
\|r_k u(x_k+r_k\cdot,T+r_k^2s)\|_3=\|u(T+r_k^2s)\|_3.
\]

At a finite singular time that norm diverges, so no rescaling sequence can
produce a globally bounded `L^infinity_s L^3_x` ancient profile. CKN supplies a
critical lower concentration, whereas compactness requires critical upper
control. Finite energy does not provide it and becomes worse under zoom.

Type-II cascades can carry an unbounded number of critical bubbles with
vanishing total energy cost. At the terminal scale they concentrate; at an
inner scale the other bubbles escape to spatial infinity, while the physical
terminal time recedes to infinite rescaled time. The exact missing property is
global `L^3` tightness/equi-integrability.

Known backward-uniqueness arguments close under strong `L^3`, finite-index
Lorentz, or nonendpoint critical Besov bounds. An arbitrary blowup profile is
not known to inherit any such global class. Assuming that inheritance is the
endpoint regularity input the route was meant to prove.

The ancient-profile tactic is therefore retired for arbitrary singularities.
No regularity or blowup result is claimed.
