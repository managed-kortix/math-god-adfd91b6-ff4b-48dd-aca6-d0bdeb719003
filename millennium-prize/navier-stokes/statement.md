# Official statement: Navier--Stokes

Primary source checked 2026-07-25: Charles Fefferman,
<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/navierstokes.pdf>.

In dimension three, prove one of the official global smooth-existence
alternatives on `R^3` or the periodic domain for all prescribed smooth data,
or one of the official forced breakdown alternatives with the stated smooth,
decaying/periodic hypotheses. Weak existence, conditional criteria, altered
equations, or numerical blowup are not solutions.
