# Cycle 272: Kida-tail duplicate audit

## Verdict: AN INFINITE-SUPPORT TOKEN IS NOT A NEW ARCHITECTURE

Let `K_a` be any member of the retired Cycle 265--267 Kida--Pelz/tangent
production family, after the already harmless operations of amplitude scaling,
translation, rotation by an admitted torus symmetry, Galilean translation, and
time reversal. A proposal of the form

\[
 u_{a,\epsilon}=K_a+r_{a,\epsilon},\qquad
 0<\|r_{a,\epsilon}\|_{A_\rho}\leq\epsilon,              \tag{272.1}
\]

does not become a new architecture merely because every nonzero
`r_(a,epsilon)` has infinitely many Fourier coefficients. Fourier support is a
regularity datum, not a production mechanism. In particular, appending a
geometric, exponential, or otherwise analytic tail to evade the word
"finite-support" is prohibited duplicate/tuning when the Kida core still
supplies the proposed stretching, direction, trajectory lead, or endpoint
growth and the tail appears only in an error allowance.

Smallness alone is not a no-go theorem. A singularly effective tail could in
principle define a new mechanism. Novelty is decided by the deletion test
below, not by whether the support set is finite or infinite and not by whether
`epsilon` is numerically small.

## Mechanism deletion test

A proposed Kida perturbation must print, before computation:

1. an exact decomposition (272.1), including an exact formula for every tail
   coefficient and a proof of realness, divergence freedom, analyticity, and
   genuinely three-dimensional support;
2. a homotopy `u_(a,epsilon,s)=K_a+s r_(a,epsilon)`, `0<=s<=1`, and a ledger of
   every indispensable signed production, transfer, persistence, and endpoint
   inequality used by the proposal;
3. for each ledger entry, whether its favorable term is a core term, a
   core--tail interaction, or a tail--tail interaction, with directed bounds
   rather than absolute-value plausibility; and
4. the first indispensable mechanism lemma that fails at `s=0`, together with
   an exact positive margin supplied by the tail-dependent term at the printed
   witness and a closed analytic majorant for all adverse interactions and the
   generated tail on the fixed interval.

Call the tail **mechanism-removable** if, after setting `s=0`, every
indispensable favorable inequality and the proposed route to promotion still
has the same logical source, while terms containing `r` are used only as
perturbative errors, generic symmetry breaking, support certification,
resolution seeding, or parameter improvement. Equivalently for this gate, the
proposal proves no signed tail-dependent lemma whose removal destroys its
production argument. A mechanism-removable proposal is
`ND270-DUPLICATE`, even if `supp Fourier(u)` is infinite for every
`epsilon>0`.

Call the tail **mechanism-essential** only if all of the following are proved:

1. a named indispensable signed identity or inequality contains a nonzero
   core--tail or tail--tail contribution;
2. deleting the tail makes that declared lemma false or removes its strict
   favorable margin, rather than merely violating the infinite-support entry
   condition;
3. the favorable contribution is enclosed in the complete Euler quantity--in
   particular the pressure flux `mathcal P_3` or a later complete-field
   persistence/endpoint inequality--and not only in vorticity, strain, one
   Fourier projection, or a Galerkin system;
4. reverse transfer, all adverse core--tail and tail--tail interactions, and the
   whole generated tail have scale-uniform directed bounds on the predeclared
   interval; and
5. the fixed parameter box, times, margin `eta_0`, and resource manifest do not
   choose `epsilon`, a horizon, a cutoff, or a tail decay rate after observing
   an output.

This is a mechanism criterion. It does not demand that every Fourier
coefficient contribute favorably, nor does it declare every small perturbation
mathematically irrelevant. It demands an exact certificate that the new
tail-dependent interaction, rather than the retired Kida driver plus a support
decoration, carries an indispensable part of the proposed proof architecture.

## Fail-closed decision rule

Review every proposed Kida perturbation in this order.

1. Canonicalize its finite core modulo the harmless operations listed above.
   If the core is a Cycle 265--267 Kida--Pelz/tangent profile, apply the deletion
   test; changing notation or using a convergent infinite series does not avoid
   it.
2. Return **`ND270-DUPLICATE`** as soon as the tail is mechanism-removable.
   This includes continuity around a retired Kida numerical lead, a small tail
   added only to pass infinite support, and retuning old coefficients, phases,
   horizons, or cutoffs through tail parameters.
3. If mechanism-essentiality is asserted but any decomposition, signed ledger,
   deletion failure, directed tail margin, or adverse-interaction majorant is
   absent or unproved, return **`ND270-ARCH-WALL`** at the first missing item.
   Ambiguity is not admission.
4. Only a mechanism-essential proposal proceeds to all six independent
   `ND270-ADMISSION` items, including the Cycle 271 exact local complete-`L3`
   production witness. Return **`ND270-ADMIT`** only if those items also pass.

No trajectory search, resource retry, Kida screen, horizon extension, or
parameter optimization is authorized during this review. A tail-essential
admission would authorize only a separately frozen compute manifest; it would
not establish a factor-two crossing. Conversely, a duplicate or architecture
wall is an operational verdict on the proposed mechanism, not a theorem that
the perturbed Euler datum can never cross the target.

No Navier--Stokes or Millennium result is claimed.
