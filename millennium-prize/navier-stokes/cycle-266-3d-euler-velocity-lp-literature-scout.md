# Cycle 266: 3D Euler velocity `L^p` literature scout

## Verdict

The checked explicit, numerical, and rigorous 3D Euler literature does not
provide precedent for

\[
 \|u(T)\|_{L^3}>2\|u(0)\|_{L^3}.                         \tag{266.1}
\]

In fact, none of the primary Kida--Pelz, classical anti-parallel-tube, or
Hou--Luo sources checked reports a two-time ratio for any undifferentiated
velocity `L^p` norm with `p != 2`. Their large-growth observables are maximum
vorticity, enstrophy, enstrophy production, strain, derivatives, support
diameter, or analyticity width. Smooth Euler conserves velocity `L^2`; none of
those positive-order or localized quantities gives a lower bound for velocity
`L^3`.

There is one useful correction to the candidate strategy. The most reusable
fully printed deterministic datum among these sources is Hou--Luo's
axisymmetric-swirl datum, not a loosely specified Gaussian Kida--Pelz-like
vorticity field. It is nevertheless a bounded-cylinder, wall-driven candidate,
not a periodic-torus seed, and its spectacular `3*10^8` reported factor is
maximum vorticity growth, not velocity growth. The rigorous anti-parallel-ring
theorems of Choi--Jeong and Gustafson--Miller--Tsai are valuable mechanism and
sanity-check sources, but they likewise prove vorticity/impulse growth rather
than (266.1).

The source-backed recommendation is therefore:

> Keep the exact trigonometric Kida datum already frozen in Cycle 265 as the
> primary periodic reproducibility baseline, but do not privilege the
> Kida--Pelz singularity literature as evidence for velocity-`L3` growth. Add
> one bounded, non-adaptive numerical comparator based on the printed
> Hou--Luo datum only if the certificate infrastructure is extended to a solid
> cylinder. Rank and promote exclusively by complete velocity-`L3` endpoint
> ratio. Do not use peak vorticity, enstrophy, strain, or apparent blowup as a
> surrogate. Treat a factor-two crossing as a new observation requiring an
> independent full-PDE enclosure; no checked source supplies precedent.

## Quantity firewall

For smooth 3D Euler on a fixed domain,

\[
 \|u(t)\|_2=\|u(0)\|_2,
 \qquad \omega=\nabla\times u.                            \tag{266.2}
\]

Vorticity growth is derivative growth. On the periodic torus, a velocity mode
`u_N=a e sin(Nx)` has

\[
 \|u_N\|_3\asymp |a|,
 \qquad \|\omega_N\|_p\asymp N|a|.                       \tag{266.3}
\]

Thus arbitrarily large vorticity, enstrophy, or strain can coexist with a
fixed or small velocity `L^3` norm. Conversely, conservation of `L^2` neither
forces nor forbids `L^3` growth: `L^3` measures spatial concentration of
velocity at fixed energy. A source is relevant to (266.1) only if it evaluates
the full velocity field in the same `L^3` norm at both endpoints. None below
does so.

This distinction also blocks a tempting inference from a singularity fit. Even
if `||omega||_infinity` behaved like `(T-t)^-1`, it would not determine the
volume of the active region or the low-frequency velocity generated by it.
Velocity `L^3` cannot be reconstructed from the peak alone.

## Source-by-source audit

### Kida--Pelz high-symmetry flows

Kida's *Three-Dimensional Periodic Flows with High-Symmetry*, J. Phys. Soc.
Japan 54 (1985), 2132--2136, DOI `10.1143/JPSJ.54.2132`, gives a deterministic
periodic high-symmetry class. The program's exact trigonometric Kida--Pelz
representative already used in Cycle 265,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 K_1&=\sin x(\cos3y\cos z-\cos y\cos3z),\\
 K_2&=\sin y(\cos3z\cos x-\cos z\cos3x),\\
 K_3&=\sin z(\cos3x\cos y-\cos x\cos3y),
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{266.4}
\]

is therefore an appropriate exact periodic baseline. Its finite Fourier
support, exact symmetries, and nonzero stretching are directly reusable.

The later Kida--Pelz computations do not target velocity `L^p` growth:

1. Boratav--Pelz, *Direct numerical simulation of transition to turbulence
   from a high-symmetry initial condition*, Phys. Fluids 6 (1994), 2757--2784,
   DOI `10.1063/1.868166`, reports sharp increases of local quantities, peak
   enstrophy, maximum vorticity, and analyticity-strip behavior. Its abstract
   says the computed maximum vorticity approximately follows `(T-Tc)^-1`
   shortly before breakup, then stops increasing under viscous effects. This
   is a Navier--Stokes/high-Reynolds computation, not a velocity-`L3` endpoint
   result for Euler.
2. Pelz, *Symmetry and the hydrodynamic blow-up problem*, J. Fluid Mech. 444
   (2001), 299--320, DOI `10.1017/S0022112001005298`, analyzes octahedral image
   strain, a compact vortex tube in the fundamental domain, and a proposed
   pointwise collapse. It gives no velocity-`L3` ratio.
3. Cichowlas--Brachet, *Evolution of complex singularities in Kida--Pelz and
   Taylor--Green inviscid flows*, Fluid Dyn. Res. 36 (2005), 239--248, DOI
   `10.1016/j.fluiddyn.2004.09.005`, tracks analyticity/complex singularities,
   not undifferentiated velocity norms.
4. Grafke--Homann--Dreher--Grauer, *Numerical simulations of possible finite
   time singularities in the incompressible Euler equations: comparison of
   numerical methods*, Physica D 237 (2008), 1932--1936, DOI
   `10.1016/j.physd.2007.11.006`, arXiv:`0711.2284`, uses a **Kida--Pelz-like**
   twelve-vortex initial condition with a Gaussian vorticity shape. It compares
   schemes using maximum vorticity and energy spectra. Agreement ends near
   `t=0.4` at `512^3` and `t=0.47` at `1024^3`; the AMR run reaches an effective
   `4096^3`, and the proposed singular time `0.638` comes from a maximum-
   vorticity scaling plot. The paper explicitly warns that late growth is
   resolution-sensitive. It neither prints a velocity-`L3` diagnostic nor
   supplies a coefficient-complete datum as convenient as (266.4).

Reusable status: **yes for (266.4), symmetry reduction, and solver stress
tests; no as evidence of large velocity `L^3` growth.** A Gaussian
Kida--Pelz-like tube should not be reconstructed from prose and then called the
published datum. If used, every centerline, core profile, amplitude,
periodization, and symmetry image must be frozen independently.

### Classical anti-parallel vortex tubes

Kerr, *Evidence for a singularity of the three-dimensional, incompressible
Euler equations*, Phys. Fluids A 5 (1993), 1725--1746, DOI
`10.1063/1.858849`, computes smooth perturbed anti-parallel tubes in a periodic
box. The reported growth laws concern peak vorticity, peak axial strain,
enstrophy production, logarithmic enstrophy growth, and an energy spectrum
approaching `k^-3`. The word "energy" here does not mean growth of total kinetic
energy, which smooth Euler conserves, and the spectrum does not supply the
missing velocity-`L3` endpoint integral.

This family is also historically non-robust as a singularity claim. Hou--Li,
*Computing nearly singular solutions using pseudo-spectral methods*, J. Comput.
Phys. 226 (2007), 379--397, DOI `10.1016/j.jcp.2007.04.014`, and Kerr's review
*Computational Euler History*, arXiv:`physics/0607148`, record that higher-
resolution/filtering calculations called the earlier long-time singular
interpretation into question. That disagreement is about vorticity resolution,
not about velocity `L^3`; neither side reports (266.1).

The rigorous modern phrase "anti-parallel vortex tube" often denotes a
different and much more structured class: axisymmetric, swirl-free,
odd-reflected vortex rings on `R^3`.

- Choi--Jeong, *On vortex stretching for anti-parallel axisymmetric flows*,
  Amer. J. Math. 147 (2025), 1251--1284, DOI
  `10.1353/ajm.2025.a971091`, arXiv:`2110.09079`, proves monotonic and infinite
  growth of a vorticity impulse and derives lower bounds for **vorticity**
  `L^p` norms for suitable data. For some compactly supported `C^{1,gamma}`
  vorticities it proves infinite-time `||omega||_infinity` growth; for a
  `C_c^infinity` datum it proves unbounded vorticity `C^alpha` norm. The paper
  conserves kinetic energy and never converts these claims to velocity `L^3`.
- Gustafson--Miller--Tsai, *Growth Rates for Anti-Parallel Vortex Tube Euler
  Flows in Three and Higher Dimensions*, J. Math. Fluid Mech. 28 (2026), Paper
  19, DOI `10.1007/s00021-026-01001-0`, arXiv:`2303.12043`, improves lower
  growth bounds for the same axisymmetric swirl-free class. Its target remains
  vorticity/support growth, not undifferentiated velocity `L^p`.

These theorems are not even indirect factor-two precedents. Their constants are
datum-dependent, the growth can occur at arbitrarily large time while energy
stays fixed, and no lower estimate for `||u(t)||_3` is stated. They are useful
for choosing sign, odd symmetry, and colliding-ring geometry and for validating
vorticity dynamics, but not for the production score.

Reusable status: **conditional.** The Choi--Jeong admissible class can yield
explicit smooth bump data after independently freezing one bump formula and
checking every sign, support, and reciprocal-integrability hypothesis. Such a
choice would be on `R^3`, not the torus. Kerr's tube geometry is a useful
historical benchmark but is less deterministic from modern source text than
the printed Hou--Luo datum below.

### Hou--Luo axisymmetric flow with swirl

Luo--Hou, *Potentially singular solutions of the 3D axisymmetric Euler
equations*, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 111 (2014), 12968--12973, DOI
`10.1073/pnas.1405238111`, arXiv:`1310.0497`, is the cleanest directly reusable
numerical datum in this audit. On

\[
 0\le r\le1,\qquad z\in\mathbb R/(L\mathbb Z),\qquad L=\frac16,
\]

with no flow through `r=1`, it introduces
`u_1=u^theta/r`, `omega_1=omega^theta/r`, and `psi_1=psi^theta/r` and prints

\[
 u_1(0;r,z)=100e^{-30(1-r^2)^4}\sin(2\pi z/L),
 \qquad \omega_1(0;r,z)=\psi_1(0;r,z)=0.                 \tag{266.5}
\]

Thus initially

\[
 u^\theta(0;r,z)=100r e^{-30(1-r^2)^4}\sin(2\pi z/L),
 \qquad u^r(0)=u^z(0)=0.                                \tag{266.6}
\]

The source reports evolution to `0.003505`, predicts a possible singular time
about `0.0035056`, and reports a `3*10^8`-fold increase in
`||omega||_infinity` with pointwise relative vorticity error of order `10^-4`.
Those numbers concern vorticity and a potential boundary singularity. They do
not report velocity `L^3`; the initial field (266.6) is already concentrated
near the wall, so even the direction of its complete velocity-`L3` change must
be computed rather than inferred.

There are three transfer mismatches:

1. The domain is a solid cylinder with a physical wall, not `T^3`.
2. The proposed singular mechanism occurs at the intersection of the wall and
   a symmetry plane; periodicizing the radial direction changes the mechanism.
3. The adaptive blowup computation is not a full-PDE interval certificate of a
   velocity-`L3` endpoint ratio.

Reusable status: **best bounded-domain comparator, not a torus seed.** Freeze
the printed `L=1/6` and normalization, evaluate (266.6) directly, retain the
no-flow wall, and predeclare a horizon strictly before the near-singular
regime. Do not transplant (266.5) to a periodic cube and retain the Hou--Luo
label.

## Factor-two precedent assessment

The search found no source-backed numerical or rigorous claim that a smooth 3D
Euler velocity satisfies

\[
 \frac{\|u(t_2)\|_{L^3}}{\|u(t_1)\|_{L^3}}>2             \tag{266.7}
\]

on a periodic box, a cylinder, or `R^3`. It also found no printed same-norm
factor-two result for undifferentiated velocity `L^p`, `p>2`, in the audited
families. This is a bounded literature non-supply statement, not a proof that
no such computation exists.

The absence is unsurprising. The singularity literature is organized around
the Beale--Kato--Majda quantity `||omega||_infinity`, enstrophy, strain, and
spectral/analyticity diagnostics. Velocity `L^3` is neither conserved nor a
standard blowup diagnostic. A plot of maximum vorticity increasing by eight
orders of magnitude and a velocity-`L3` ratio close to one are logically
compatible.

Accordingly, a future factor-two numerical crossing would have novelty as a
diagnostic observation within this literature. It would still be only a
numerical lead until the full Euler trajectory, complete velocity endpoint
cubature, and analytic tail are enclosed. No singularity or Navier--Stokes
conclusion follows from the crossing alone.

## Deterministic-data ranking and recommendation

| candidate | exact/reusable specification | domain match | velocity-`L3` evidence | disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kida trigonometric field (266.4) | finite Fourier, exact coefficients and symmetries | exact `T^3` match | none in sources | keep as baseline; bounded phase/tangent variants only |
| Hou--Luo datum (266.5) | explicit smooth scalar formula and boundary conditions | cylinder with wall | none | best separate comparator if wall certificate is built |
| Choi--Jeong anti-parallel class | rigorous sign/support template; one bump still must be frozen | `R^3`, swirl-free | vorticity only | use for mechanism validation, not promotion |
| Kerr anti-parallel tubes | smooth periodic geometry but many implementation choices | periodic box | vorticity/enstrophy only | historical benchmark; lower reproducibility priority |
| Grafke et al. Gaussian Kida--Pelz-like field | qualitative profile in audited text | periodic box | vorticity only | do not reconstruct as a production datum |

The immediate production rule should be:

1. Keep one exact Kida member as an unmodified baseline before running the 132
   Cycle 265 perturbations. Compute forward and reversed endpoint ratios at a
   frozen horizon grid, but promote only one predeclared orientation per member.
2. Record `||u(t)||_3`, `||u(t)||_4`, and `||u(t)||_infinity` as velocity
   diagnostics, with `L^3` alone controlling the gate. Record vorticity and
   strain in a separate diagnostic block labelled non-promotional.
3. If a cylinder solver is added, run exactly (266.5) as a separate bounded
   comparator with independent mesh refinement and complete 3D cylindrical
   quadrature for `int |u|^3 r dr dtheta dz`. Do not merge its score with the
   torus family.
4. Do not infer velocity concentration from a vorticity singularity fit,
   support expansion, enstrophy increase, or spectrum. Require the observed
   ratio itself to clear the existing numerical lead gate and then the
   full-PDE certificate.
5. If all deterministic baselines remain near one, stop this mechanism-guided
   family. The audited literature supplies no reason to relax the factor-two
   gate or to broaden around peak-vorticity winners.
