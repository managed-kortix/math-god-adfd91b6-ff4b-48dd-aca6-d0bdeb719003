# Hostile audit

The critical mild kernel is nonintegrable at the endpoint and the pressure
term has no sign. A finite Galerkin trajectory is not a PDE certificate.
Reject hidden Serrin assumptions, unresolved-mode errors, compactness without
strong convergence, and constants depending on cutoff, frequency, or the
unknown supremum norm.

Do not confuse finite support at the initial time with an Euler trajectory
remaining in fixed finite support. Kishimoto--Yoneda forbids the latter, not
the former. Admit finite launch data only with a full analytic majorant for all
generated modes; an initial-tail bound or Galerkin cutoff is insufficient.

For every Kida--Pelz perturbation, ignore the finite/infinite support label and
apply the mechanism deletion test in
`cycle-272-kida-tail-duplicate-audit.md`. A tail used only as an error,
symmetry breaker, support certificate, numerical seed, or tuned parameter is a
duplicate. Missing a signed tail-essential lemma is an architecture wall, not
permission to compute.
