# Cycle 254: zero-circulation patch dynamics and the smooth-transfer wall

## Verdict: CONDITIONAL TRANSFER, DYNAMICAL CERTIFICATE MISSING

Contour dynamics can reduce the requested search to an exact finite-time patch
certificate, and a strict patch velocity-`L^3` gap then transfers to smooth
vorticity quantitatively. The smoothing step is not the wall. No deterministic
periodic opposite-sign patch pair is presently enclosed here with a rigorous
non-relative factor-two change of the complete Biot--Savart velocity norm.

Kirchhoff/Kida elliptical closure does not give the needed unforced pair. A
single Kirchhoff ellipse is a relative equilibrium. For two finite separated
patches, the field generated by one is not affine on the other; higher Taylor
terms generate boundary harmonics beyond the ellipse manifold. Keeping only
translation and linear strain is an asymptotic model, not exact Euler dynamics.
Symmetric translating or rotating pairs are again relative equilibria and have
constant complete `L^3` norm.

No factor-two patch orbit, smooth Euler orbit, Navier--Stokes result, or
Millennium solution is claimed.

## Deterministic periodic family

Work on normalized `T^2=(R/2 pi Z)^2`. Fix rational parameters

\[
 0<b<a<d/4,\qquad d<\pi/2,
\]

and let `E(a,b)` be the centered ellipse with semiaxes `a,b`. Define

\[
 D_+^0=(d,0)+E(a,b),\qquad
 D_-^0=(-d,0)+R_\theta E(a,b),                         \tag{254.1}
\]

where `theta/pi` is rational, and

\[
 \omega^P_0=\Omega(1_{D_+^0}-1_{D_-^0}),\qquad
 \Omega\in\mathbb Q\setminus\{0\}.                   \tag{254.2}
\]

This is deterministic, periodic, bounded, and exactly mean zero. Its Yudovich
solution is unique. As long as boundaries are disjoint, positively oriented
parametrizations obey periodic contour dynamics

\[
 \partial_t z_i(\alpha)=
 \Omega\sum_{j\in\{+,-\}}\sigma_j
 \int_{\mathbb T}G_{\mathbb T^2}(z_i(\alpha)-z_j(\beta))
 \partial_\beta z_j(\beta)\,d\beta
 +c_i(\alpha)\partial_\alpha z_i,                     \tag{254.3}
\]

where `sigma_+=1`, `sigma_-=-1`, `G_T2` is the periodic zero-mean Green
function, the self term is principal value, and `c_i` is tangential gauge. A
different `nabla^perp` convention changes the global sign. Equations
(254.1)--(254.3), not an ellipse moment closure, define the honest candidate.

## Why exact elliptical dynamics does not close

Near `D_+`, the velocity of the separated patch `D_-` is analytic:

\[
 u_-(c_++\xi)=U_0+A\xi+\sum_{m\ge2}U_m[\xi^{\otimes m}]. \tag{254.4}
\]

The constant and trace-free linear terms produce translation and Kida-type
strain. A finite patch is neither a point vortex nor a uniform external strain;
generically its periodic multipole expansion has nonzero higher derivatives.
On an elliptic boundary these force normal Fourier modes `|k|>=3`. Thus the
two-ellipse ODE is at best controlled when `rho=max(a,b)/d` is small.

This creates a quantitative tension: for `rho<<1`, neglected multipoles and
shape changes are perturbative on an `O(1)` interaction interval. An order-one
norm change requires a regime where the full contour equation must be enclosed.

## Patch-to-smooth transfer theorem

**Lemma.** Let `omega^P` be a bounded mean-zero toral Euler solution on `[0,T]`
and suppose

\[
 A_0=\|K\omega^P(0)\|_3>0,\qquad
 \|K\omega^P(T)\|_3\ge(2+\eta)A_0                    \tag{254.5}
\]

for `eta>0`. Then a smooth mean-zero Euler solution `omega^epsilon` exists with

\[
 \|K\omega^\epsilon(T)\|_3>2\|K\omega^\epsilon(0)\|_3. \tag{254.6}
\]

It suffices that

\[
 \max_{t\in\{0,T\}}
 \|K(\omega^\epsilon(t)-\omega^P(t))\|_3
 < {\eta A_0\over3}.                                  \tag{254.7}
\]

Indeed, if the left side is `delta`, then

\[
 {\|K\omega^\epsilon(T)\|_3\over
   \|K\omega^\epsilon(0)\|_3}
 \ge { (2+\eta)A_0-\delta\over A_0+\delta}>2
\]

exactly when `3 delta<eta A_0`.

Take `omega^epsilon_0=rho_epsilon*omega^P_0`; convolution preserves the mean.
These data are smooth and uniformly bounded in `L^infty`, hence generate global
smooth 2D Euler solutions. Standard Yudovich compactness and uniqueness give,
on every fixed interval,

\[
 \omega^\epsilon\longrightarrow\omega^P
 \quad\hbox{in }C([0,T];L^2).                          \tag{254.8}
\]

For mean-zero `f`, Sobolev and the Fourier multiplier estimate give

\[
 \|Kf\|_6\le C_S\|f\|_2,
 \qquad \|Kf\|_2\le\|f\|_2.                           \tag{254.9}
\]

Interpolation yields the quantitative bridge

\[
 \boxed{\ \|Kf\|_3\le C_S^{1/2}\|f\|_2.\ }            \tag{254.10}
\]

Equations (254.8)--(254.10) imply (254.7) for sufficiently small `epsilon`.
This uses fixed-time Yudovich stability and smoothing by `K`, not classical
patch-boundary stability or high-norm estimates for the mollified data.

For a printed numerical threshold on `epsilon`, qualitative convergence must
be replaced by an explicit Osgood stability modulus and initial mollification
error. That is bookkeeping after a strict margin in (254.5), not the current
dynamical bottleneck.

## Exact contour certificate required

A valid production packet must provide:

1. Fixed rational/algebraic parameters, orientations, and Green normalization.
2. An a posteriori enclosure proving embeddedness, chord--arc bounds,
   inter-patch separation, and uniqueness-class regularity on `[0,T]`.
3. Outward-rounded cubature of the complete periodic velocity proving (254.5),
   not cubature of a point-vortex or affine-strain surrogate.
4. A non-relative-shape witness, such as a separated change in a normalized
   boundary mode `|k|>=3` after quotienting translations and rotations.
5. Independent recomputation of the contour residual and endpoint integrals.

Bounded patch vorticity gives finite velocity `L^3`. On the torus there is no
far-field divergence; zero net circulation is required to invert the Laplacian
in the mean-zero Biot--Savart convention.

## Quantitative wall

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{strict factor-two patch certificate}
 \Longrightarrow
 \text{smooth-vorticity factor-two Euler orbit}.}
\]

The implication follows from (254.7)--(254.10). Its premise is absent. No exact
finite-dimensional ellipse dynamics supplies it, and no rigorous full-contour
integration for (254.1) is present. Thus `ND251` remains open at the
non-relative shape-production gate, not the smoothing gate.
