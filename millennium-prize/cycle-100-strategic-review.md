# Cycle 100: strategic review and derived-Kato calibration gate

After thirty cycles of mechanism audits, the portfolio was ranked by whether a
route still offered a non-equivalent structural production lemma.  BSD integral
Euler-system primitivity ranked first, followed by Yang--Mills physical-slab
contraction and Navier phase-sensitive depletion.  P-versus-NP magnification
remains full-reach but mechanismless; Hodge and RH routes remain dominated by
seed/equivalent-positivity gates.

## Exact BSD calibration

For
\[
 E=389\mathrm a1:y^2+y=x^3+x^2-2x,
 \quad p=5,\quad n=41\cdot61,
\]
Chan-Ho Kim already records
\[
 \widetilde\delta^{(1)}_{41\cdot61}(E)\ne0\pmod5.
\]
The local data pass exactly:
\[
 N_E=389,\quad c_{389}=1,\quad a_5=-3,
\]
and the residual representation at `5` is surjective.  Exact point counts give
\[
 a_{41}=-3,\qquad a_{61}=-8,
\]
so each auxiliary prime satisfies
\[
 \ell\equiv1\pmod5,\qquad a_\ell\equiv\ell+1\pmod5,
\]
with cyclotomic ideal exactly `5 Z_5`.  These are cyclotomic Kurihara primes,
not anticyclotomic Heegner Kolyvagin primes.

With primitive roots `6 mod 41` and `2 mod 61`, an independent reconstruction
reports the generator-dependent residue `4 mod 5`; changing generators rescales
by a nonzero element, so only nonvanishing is canonical.

The published implication is
\[
 \widetilde\delta_n^{(1)}\ne0
 \Longrightarrow
 \bar\kappa_n^{\mathrm{Kato}}\ne0
 \Longrightarrow
 \text{primitivity of Kato's core-rank-one Kolyvagin system}.
\]
The residual component lives in the modified Selmer quotient
\[
 H^1_{\mathcal F_{\rm can}(n)}(\mathbf Q,E[5])\otimes G_n,
\]
and its singular localization at `5` maps to the Kurihara scalar.  It is not a
rank-two Kolyvagin system.  The two auxiliary primes measure derivative order;
the rank-two statement is instead the resulting Selmer corank.  Kim already
derives
\[
 \operatorname{corank}_{\mathbf Z_5}\operatorname{Sel}(\mathbf Q,E[5^\infty])=2,
 \qquad \Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[5^\infty]=0.
\]

Thus the proposed "derived Kato rank-two primitivity" promotion was
terminologically wrong and mathematically nonnovel.  It is retained only as a
calibration of exact integral production.  No complex derivative, strong BSD
formula, higher-rank Euler system, or new BSD case follows.

## Other strategic gates

- A Gaussian explicit-formula test localized at a hypothetical off-line zero
  loses to sign-indefinite neighboring off-line quartets and an oscillatory prime
  channel of larger exponential scale.  A positive resolvent works only to the
  Euler-product boundary and cannot distinguish off-line zeros.
- Minimal initial `L^3` blowup data are known conditionally to exist, but strong
  almost-periodicity of the orbit would imply bounded trajectory `L^3`, already
  excluded by the endpoint regularity theorem.  Energy does not produce that
  compactness.
- Standard conjecture `B` algebraizes motivated operations but does not prove
  every Hodge class motivated; generation remains the missing cycle input.
- Finite-lattice Yang--Mills gaps are not monotone in coupling: an exact one-
  plaquette `Z_2` physical Hamiltonian has
  \[
  \Delta(\beta)=2\sqrt{16/\beta^2+\beta^2},
  \]
  decreasing then increasing.  Free-energy convexity cannot bridge strong and
  weak coupling.
- CJSW, small Vertex Cover, and average-case MCSP retain valid magnification
  statements, but no new selector, computation-sensitive kernel bound, or
  sample-fitting lower-bound mechanism survived review.

Cycle 100 is a strategic rotation gate, not a solution claim.
