# Cycle 285: bounded post-Kummer frontier monitoring

## Scope and gate

This is one bounded monitoring wave after the Cycle 284 cutoff.  It checks
material submitted or revised from `2026-11-03 00:00 UTC` through
`2026-11-04 13:00 UTC`, plus repository refs visible after the version-pinned
generalized-Kummer record.  Four disjoint scouts cover Hodge;
Navier--Stokes/RH; BSD/Yang--Mills; and P versus NP.  The complexity scout also
serves as a hostile eligibility sentinel.

A candidate is eligible only if it **already** supplies all four items:

1. one exact target and quantified official-facing implication or separated
   information gain;
2. all mathematical and computational inputs;
3. fixed finite outputs, resources, and a first-failure stop; and
4. novelty against every recorded terminal route.

The wave may read primary sources and repository objects.  It may not repair a
stopped route, search for a missing theorem or datum, manufacture a packet,
repeat a census, substitute a restricted model for an unrestricted one, or
call a special-family result a Clay resolution.

## Scout returns

### Hodge: `NO-INPUT`

Two post-cutoff records are mathematically adjacent but fail the first gate.

- Donovan--Wang--Zhang, `arXiv:2611.04118v1`, submitted
  `2026-11-03 00:51:28 UTC`, proves derived equivalence for birational smooth
  projective hyper-Kähler varieties of generalized Kummer type.  Derived
  equivalence is not an algebraicity theorem for rational Hodge classes and
  supplies no new universal-variety implication.
- Liu--Shen, `arXiv:2611.04471v1`, submitted
  `2026-11-03 07:59:36 UTC`, proves algebraicity and quasi-projectivity facts
  for period images, constructs Stein neighborhoods and Hodge-filtration
  realizations, and derives metric comparisons.  Algebraicity of a period
  image is not algebraicity of every rational `(p,p)` class on the source
  varieties.

Neither source supplies the missing common-model PEL chain data or a new
finite packet with a Clay-facing implication.  Neither changes the restricted
external corollary recorded in Cycle 284.

### Navier--Stokes and RH: `NO-INPUT`

The post-cutoff Navier records inspected are model-mismatched.

- `arXiv:2611.05212v1`, submitted `2026-11-03 16:59:27 UTC`, concerns
  two-dimensional Navier--Stokes with Navier friction and a vanishing-viscosity
  limit on a bounded domain.  It is not a deterministic three-dimensional
  evolutionary critical-`L^3` estimate for the Clay problem.
- `arXiv:2611.05050v1`, submitted `2026-11-03 15:16:55 UTC`, derives a
  stochastic transport-noise limit from rapidly varying random advection.  It
  does not control the deterministic periodic three-dimensional equation.

Neither supplies independent control of the stopped low-amplitude
`L^4` remainder or a complete official packet.

For RH, `arXiv:2611.04359v1`, submitted `2026-11-03 05:41:31 UTC`, is explicitly

bounded source check found no other complete post-cutoff RH packet.
