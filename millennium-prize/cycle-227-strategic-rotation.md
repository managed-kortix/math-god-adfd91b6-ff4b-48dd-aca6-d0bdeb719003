Cycle 226 closes coefficient-dependent exact finite Fourier closure for real 2D
Euler: after removal of the mean, every orbit in one fixed finite support is an
equilibrium on an origin-line or origin-circle.  Cycle 225's one frozen
leakage-controlled packet also fails its item 4 orbit threshold, reaching only
`1.000637...` instead of `9/4`.  Do not tune that packet, enlarge a Galerkin
box, search another exact cancellation variety, or replace fixed support by a
finite sequence of exact support closures.  A future Navier re-entry must
certify a genuinely infinite generated tail with scale-uniform estimates.

This retires an architecture, not the universal factor-two lemma and not any
Navier--Stokes statement.

## Selected main funnel

Rotate the main funnel to the **Yang--Mills shared-link finite-size coercivity
admission gate**.  The target is a local-to-global theorem for the
gauge-invariant Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian, formulated before any larger
spectral computation.  A candidate is admitted only if it supplies all of the
following data.

1. Fix the finite-volume boundary convention, normalization, coupling range,
   block cover, and Gauss-law physical Hilbert spaces.  Give explicit
   restriction and overlap maps between a block, two overlapping blocks, and
   the global physical space; tensor-product plaquette embeddings are forbidden.
2. State one quantitative coercivity inequality with explicit constants whose
   hypotheses involve only blocks of a declared finite size and whose
   conclusion is a volume-uniform lower bound on the full physical
   vacuum-complement gap.  The proof must account for shared-link intertwiner
   sectors and every overlap multiplicity.  It may not assume temporal decay,
   a global gap, or a stable synthesis equivalent to the conclusion.
3. Give a fail-closed finite test for every local spectral or overlap constant
   in the theorem.  Spin cutoffs require certified untruncated Casimir-tail
   enclosures; Ritz gaps alone are inadmissible.  The Cycle 226 two-plaquette
   reduction is the mandatory smallest normalization and recoupling check.
4. State the physical scaling delivered by the bound as the lattice spacing
   and bare coupling vary.  Continuum tightness, reflection positivity,
   nontriviality, and Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction remain explicit later
   gates and may not be inferred from a lattice estimate.

The admission gate has two closing outcomes.  A **production certificate** is a
proved finite-size theorem satisfying items 1--4 together with one fully
certified finite-block instance giving a strictly positive global lower bound.
An **architecture obstruction** is an exact shared-link block or volume family
violating the proposed coercivity inequality or making its required constant
degenerate.  Such an obstruction retires only the declared cover and
inequality, not all finite-size criteria.

Until one outcome is produced, do not launch larger spin-cutoff diagonalizations
or report isolated finite-volume gaps as progress on the Clay statement.  No
Yang--Mills mass gap or other Millennium result is claimed.
