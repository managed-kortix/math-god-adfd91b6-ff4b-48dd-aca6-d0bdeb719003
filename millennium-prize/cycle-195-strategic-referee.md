# Cycle 195: strategic referee after the rank-one collision twists

## Decision

Rotate the main funnel from Birch--Swinnerton--Dyer to Hodge.  Do not continue
directly from the two rank-one twist certificates into a refined-BSD campaign.

Cycles 193--194 prove exact analytic rank one for the two collision twists and
Gross--Zagier--Kolyvagin gives algebraic rank one and finite `Sha`. Cycle 195
also certifies a generator and regulator for `D=-1499`, and encloses its
point-normalized leading-term quotient near one, but does not determine the
order of `Sha` or prove refined BSD. The Cycle 190 collision also
falsifies strict governance of the Kurihara coordinate by the named field
`L_0`.  Enlarging the field by one separating bit does not restore governance,
and Cycle 191 isolates the missing input as a prime-denominator modular-symbol
correlation theorem in a nonabelian Frobenius class.

The two live BSD continuations therefore have poor main-funnel leverage:

- computing and saturating a point of predicted height about `2659.76` for
  `D=-29023` would make one rank-one result more explicit but would not supply
  the full order of `Sha` or a family theorem;
- constructing the rational integral determinant-line zeta element and
  identifying its archimedean and `p`-adic regulator realizations is the
  refined-BSD/ETNC bridge itself, not a presently separated production lemma.

The BSD artifacts remain valid bounded results and the high-height point is a
reasonable scout.  They no longer justify keeping BSD as the unique main
funnel.

## One exact gate

Promote the fixed-degree relative-Chow gate for the Cycle 151 Hodge seed.

Let `A_0=E_i^3 x E_i^3`, let `S` be the nine-dimensional local PEL base through
`A_0`, let `alpha_0=P_Weil[Gamma]`, and let

`D_0=930187500000000000`.

For `Gamma_k=Gamma_(u^k)`, restriction of the PEL polarization gives
`deg(Gamma_k)=(1+5^k)^2(1+3*5^k)`.  Thus the exact degrees of the explicit
Cycle 169 pair are

`d_+=6072151396206990896`,

`d_-=2315779370123038256`.

Work in the finite-type ambient product of relative Chow spaces of effective
three-cycles of degrees `d_+` and `d_-` over `S`.  As corrected in Cycle 196,
fixed endpoint degrees do not make the rational-equivalence locus itself finite
type.

The gate is to produce one special-fiber pair `(Y^+,Y^-)`, with an explicit
finite rational-equivalence witness, having all of the following properties:

1. `[Y^+]-[Y^-]=D_0 alpha_0` in `CH^3(A_0)`, certified by an explicit rational
   equivalence;
2. the differential from the relative Chow germ at `(Y^+,Y^-)` to `T_0S` has
   rank nine;
3. a rank-nine first-order lift extends over
   `C[t_1,...,t_9]/(t_1,...,t_9)^3`, so its primary second-order obstruction
   vanishes.

This is one bounded, non-equivalent production test.  The graph expansion is
already excluded: Cycle 169 gives it rank zero, and Cycle 170 excludes replacing
the signed class by one effective cycle.  Success would exhibit genuinely new
moving Chow support and justify a subsequent formal-smoothness/algebraization
gate.  A negative alternative must additionally bound the auxiliary-cycle,
rational-chain, and map-degree data uniformly, or obstruct every finite-type
witness stratum; connected Chow components give only algebraic equivalence.
Such an obstruction would retire the smallest fixed-degree repair without
asserting the Hodge conjecture.

No Millennium result is claimed.
