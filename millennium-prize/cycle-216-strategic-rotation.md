# Cycle 216: strategic rotation after the frozen Navier screens

## Retirement decision

Retire the frozen max-wave-four Euler screen as the main-funnel architecture.
The Cycle 214 budget is exhausted, its best coarse ratio is not spatially
resolved, and the resolved ratio is only about `1.014`.  The exact Cycle 215
enclosure pipeline is a genuine certification advance, but its enclosed datum
decays in endpoint `L^3` and supplies no factor-two crossing.  A validator is
not a production mechanism.

This applies the Cycle 210 rotation rule narrowly.  Do not enlarge the Fourier
box, add random starts, or spend more resolution merely because the existing
screen missed.  A Navier campaign may re-enter only with a structural mechanism
for greater critical-norm transfer, such as a specified coupled inverse-transfer
cascade, and a coarse-to-fine candidate with enough margin to absorb a full-PDE
tail enclosure.  The universal factor-two lemma itself is not refuted or
retired; only the frozen low-mode screening architecture is retired.
