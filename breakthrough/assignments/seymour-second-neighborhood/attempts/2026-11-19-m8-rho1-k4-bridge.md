# Bridge and checked composition for the rooted `m=8, rho=1, k_T=4` cell

## Scope

This note bridges one exact rooted cell of an order-18 minimal
counterexample to the 16-vertex simultaneous-source projection certified by
`experiments/k4-simsrc-package.md`.  Here `m` is the total number of missing
unordered pairs, `rho=e(C,B)`, and `k_T` is the number of holes in `T`.  The
letter `K` below denotes a set of B vertices and is not `k_T`.

The conclusion is only that this conditional cell is empty.  It is not a
closure of all `m=8`, all order 18, or SNC.

## Lemma (full cell implies the certified projection)

Assume the known theorem that every oriented graph with minimum outdegree at
most seven satisfies SNC.  Let D be a counterexample chosen first with minimum
order and then minimum number of arcs.  Suppose D has 18 vertices, eight
missing pairs, and the robust-root cell has `rho=1,k_T=4`.  Then, after
permuting two color classes, `D[T]` extends to a model of `build(6)` or
`build(7)` in `k4_simsrc_cnf.py`.

### 1. Degrees and robust root

D has `C(18,2)-8=145` arcs.  Since every outdegree is at least eight, exactly
one vertex r has outdegree nine and all others have outdegree eight.

By vertex minimality, D-r has a Seymour vertex v.  Necessarily `v->r`; if not,
its first neighborhood is unchanged and its second neighborhood cannot shrink
when r is restored, making it Seymour in D.  Now

```
7=d+_{D-r}(v) <= d++_{D-r}(v) <= d++_D(v) <= d+_D(v)-1=7.
```

Thus all inequalities are equalities and
`N++_{D-r}(v)=N++_D(v)`.  Put

```
A=N+(v),  B=N++(v),
C=V(D) minus ({v} union A union B),
A'=A minus {r},  T={v} union A union B.
```

Then `|A|=8, |B|=7, |C|=2, |A'|=7`, and every b in B has an A'
predecessor (its two-walk from v survives deletion of r).  No vertex of A
points to C, or it would put that C vertex in B.  By definition v also does
not point to C.

### 2. Hole and C-row ledger

Let pi count holes between C and `{v} union A`, including the possible
internal C-hole, and let

```
e = h(T)+h(C,B).
```

The eight holes partition as `pi+e=8`.  Summing the two degree-eight C rows,
all present pairs from C to `{v} union A` point out of C, and the internal
C-pair contributes one outgoing arc if present.  Hence

```
16 = 19-pi+rho,
pi-rho=3,  e+rho=5.
```

For `rho=1`, `e=4`.  Since `k_T=h(T)=4`, there is no C--B hole.  Of the
fourteen present B--C pairs, one points C-to-B and thirteen point B-to-C.
Therefore exactly six vertices dominate both C vertices; call them K.  The
remaining vertex b0 dominates exactly one C vertex.

Relabel

```
v=0, r=1, A'={2,...,8}, K={9,...,14}, b0=15.
```

All A' outneighbors lie in T.  Counting the C outneighbors of K and b0 gives
the exact T-outdegree profile

```
8, 9, 8^7, 6^6, 7,
```

and `N_T+(v)={r} union A'`.  These are precisely the trichotomy, four-hole,
root-row, degree, and robust-column clauses of the projection.

### 3. Exact predecessor union

Define

```
P={a in A' : a->b for some b in K},  p=|P|.
```

Every K column is hit, every member of P hits a K column, and no member of
`A' minus P` hits K.  If x lies outside P, then x cannot point to v, K, or C.
Its only possible outneighbors are r, the other six A' vertices, and b0:
exactly eight vertices.  Since `d+(x)=8`, x dominates all eight.  Two such
outsiders would form a digon.  Consequently `|A' minus P|<=1`, so `p` is six
or seven.  Relabel A' so that P is its first p vertices.  This gives exactly
the producer's P clauses.

### 4. Simultaneous inaccessible pairs

Fix a in P and choose b in K with `a->b`.  Both C vertices are exact second
neighbors of a through b, while a has all eight outneighbors in T.  Let
`S_a={a} union N_T+(a)`.  Seven T vertices lie outside S_a.  Since a is bad,
`d++(a)<=7`; the two C vertices already consume two exact second-neighbor
slots, so at most five of those seven T vertices are reached by a T-two-walk.
Choose two distinct inaccessible endpoints.

For a selected endpoint u, `a` does not point to u; every present source-u
pair points `u->a`; and for each x with `a->x`, every present x-u pair points
`u->x`, excluding `a->x->u`.  Under exact pair trichotomy these are exactly
the selector guards in the projection.  Choosing one pair for every a in P
extends the graph assignment to all selector variables; the standard canonical
extensions satisfy the exact-cardinality auxiliaries.

This proves the lemma.

## Certified finite composition

For p=6, the stabilizer of source 2 has 17 pair orbits covering all 105
possible selector pairs; every representative leaf has a checked LRAT.  For
p=7 there are 12 source-2 orbits: 11 have direct checked LRATs and the hard
orbit `{1,3}` is exhaustively split into 17 source-3 orbits, all checked.
`k4_simsrc_split.py` verifies these covers.

The retained package contains 45 LRATs.  On 2026-11-19 a fresh replay rebuilt
`lrat-check.c` from pinned drat-trim commit
`2e3b2dc0ecf938addbd779d42877b6ed69d9a985`, confirmed source SHA-256
`bf07c2ac96b9035da1ebcc578cb95e956a2b795629d613154cdb307f8a8f4a95`,
regenerated and independently reconstructed every CNF, authenticated every
compressed and raw proof identity, and obtained exactly one `c VERIFIED` for
all 45 rows.

The current compiler produced a 22,144-byte checker with SHA-256
`9b6efb6ec006e7450edac28d26e240b431dd4a38dd2b00df1a4be29f3d990861`,
not the frozen historical 22,064-byte ELF recorded by the package.  Therefore
the unmodified bit-identity wrapper correctly fails its historical binary gate;
the replay above is explicitly a source-identical checker audit, not a claim
that the historical ELF was reproduced.  The LRAT content passed.

## Theorem

There is no vertex-minimal, then arc-minimal, order-18 SNC counterexample with
eight missing pairs whose robust unique-high-vertex root lies in the exact
cell `rho=1,k_T=4`.

Indeed, the bridge would produce a model of the p=6 or p=7 projection, whereas
the checked exhaustive LRAT package refutes both.  This theorem remains a
finite conditional exclusion and is not a solution of SNC.
