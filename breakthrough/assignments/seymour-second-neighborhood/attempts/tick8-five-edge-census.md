The earlier focus on `P6` alone was incomplete. Up to isolated vertices, 26
unlabelled simple graphs have five edges. Verified missing-graph theorems cover
all but exactly

```
P6, P4 disjoint P3, C3 disjoint P3,
C4 disjoint K2, 2P3 disjoint K2.                     (1)
```

`P6` is the unique connected uncovered type. The four disconnected types are
not generalized combs (each contains an induced `2K2` or another forbidden
subgraph), and the disjoint-path theorem is conditional where applicable.

For the B6 root branch, exact C-layer placement and immediate arc-minimality
reduce the four disconnected types respectively to 50, 17, 11, and 21
canonical local ledgers: 99 total. Together with the 71 `P6` ledgers, the exact
five-edge B6 frontier has 170 canonical local ledgers before full gain/loss
incidence completion.

For B7, the four disconnected types reduce to 21 canonical choices of the two
remainder vertices before assigning all layer incidences. No whole shape is
eliminated by scalar equations alone.

Therefore any certified five-edge computation must cover all five shapes in
(1), not only `P6`.
