# Two pentagons joined by a bridge, with arbitrary attached trees

## Theorem

Let `G` be obtained from two vertex-disjoint copies of `C5` by adding one edge
between them and attaching arbitrary rooted trees at arbitrary core vertices.
Then, for the continuous Coulson phase

`Theta_G(t) = Arg(i^(-n) phi_G(it))`, `t>0`,

one has the pointwise sharp comparison

`0 < Theta_G(t) <= Theta_H(t) < pi`,                         (1)

where `H` is the bare ten-vertex bridge core. Equality throughout (1) occurs
for the bare core. Consequently

`integral_0^infinity t Theta_G(t) dt`
` <= integral_0^infinity t Theta_H(t) dt < pi/2`,             (2)

and

`s^+(G) >= n + s^+(H)-10 > n`.                               (3)

Thus bridge separation of the two pentagons remains safe under completely
arbitrary tree attachments. The assertion is not a diagonal-penalty theorem:
it uses the special matching activities generated by trees.

## 1. Tree elimination

For a forest `F`, let

`Z_F(t) = sum_M t^(|V(F)|-2|M|)`.

Orient every attached branch toward its core vertex. Its matching message is

`m_(u->v) = Z_(T_(u->v))(t)/Z_(T_(u->v)-u)(t)`
`           = t + sum_(w child of u) 1/m_(w->u) >= t`.

After extracting a common positive branch factor `K(t)`, every core vertex
has an effective activity

`a_v(t) = t + y_v(t)`, `y_v(t)>=0`.                           (4)

The same `K(t)` is present in every Sachs carrier, including a carrier in
which a whole pentagon is deleted. Hence it has no effect on phase.

## 2. Exact bridge formula

For the left weighted pentagon, rooted at the bridge endpoint, put

`p = Z_(C5-root)(a)`, `q = Z_C5(a)`;

for the right one put

`s = Z_(C5-root)(a)`, `r = Z_C5(a)`.

These are signless weighted matching partitions. Splitting matchings according
to use of the bridge gives `qr+ps`: `qr` is the contribution without the
bridge and `ps` is its contribution with both endpoints deleted. Each `C5`
has Sachs multiplier `2i`. If the left cycle is selected, the surviving
carrier is the full right pentagon, namely `r`; if the right is selected, it
is `q`. The cycles are disjoint, so selecting both contributes
`(2i)^2=-4`. Therefore the exact formula is

`Psi_G(t)/K(t) = (qr+ps-4) + 2i(q+r).`                        (5)

This verifies the labels and both deletion terms in the proposed formula.
In particular the imaginary part is positive, but the real part is negative
near zero; the phase must be treated in `(0,pi)`, not by an unqualified
principal `atan` ratio.

For one rooted weighted pentagon with activities `(a0,x1,x2,x3,x4)`, define

`P = x1*x2*x3*x4 + x3*x4 + x1*x4 + x1*x2 + 1`,

`B = x2*x3*x4+x2+x4 + x1*x2*x3+x1+x3`.

Then its deleted-root and full partitions are

`p=P`, `q=a0*P+B`.                                           (6)

For the bare pentagon these specialize to

`P0=t^4+3t^2+1`, `Q0=t^5+5t^3+5t`.                          (7)

Thus the bare phase is explicitly

`Theta_H(t)=atan2(4Q0, Q0^2+P0^2-4)`.                        (8)

Formula (8), with `atan2` valued in `(0,pi)`, is also a closed pointwise
majorant. A single ordinary `atan` expression is invalid where its denominator
is negative. In fact

`Q0^2+P0^2-4=t^10+11t^8+41t^6+61t^4+31t^2-3`,

which is negative for small positive `t` (and has its unique positive zero at
`t=0.287385...`). Thus no globally valid nonnegative rational bound obtained
by merely replacing `atan u` with `u` can use this denominator. The exact bare
`atan2` majorant is the useful global substitute.

## 3. Coefficientwise phase certificate

Write

`X=qr+ps-4`, `Y=2(q+r)`,

`X0=Q0^2+P0^2-4`, `Y0=4Q0`.

The desired angular comparison is certified by

`Y0*X-Y*X0 >= 0`, or equivalently

`2Q0(qr+ps-4) - (q+r)(Q0^2+P0^2-4) >= 0`.                   (9)

Indeed, substitute `a_v=t+y_v` at all ten vertices and expand the left side
of (9) in `t,y_0,...,y_9`. Exact integer polynomial arithmetic gives:

- 4891 nonzero monomials;
- every nonzero coefficient is positive, with minimum 1 and maximum 289;
- every monomial contains at least one `y_v`, so the bare specialization is
  exactly zero;
- the canonical ordered term stream has SHA-256 digest
  `365e18dedf9032fbcdb88af83d033f0651f02412c796b4f1dfde04152a478af1`.

Since both imaginary parts are positive, both angles lie in `(0,pi)`. Their
difference lies in `(-pi,pi)`, and (9) says

`sin(Theta_G-Theta_H) <= 0`.

It follows without any assumption on the signs of `X` and `X0` that
`Theta_G<=Theta_H`. This proves (1). It also proves that minimum activity
`a_v=t` maximizes the phase pointwise among all activities of the form
`a_v>=t`; indeed the inequality is strict as soon as any `y_v>0`, because
each variable occurs in a positive pure-variable monomial. Numerical
monotonicity is replaced by an exact coefficientwise certificate.

## 4. Exact integral of the majorant

The bare characteristic polynomial is

`phi_H(x)=(x-1)(x^2-x-3)(x^2+x-1)^2(x^3-4x+1)`.              (10)

Let `rho` be the positive root in `(2,sqrt(5))` of

`rho^3-4rho-1=0`.

The negative root of the cubic factor in (10) is `-rho`. The sum of squares
of its two positive roots is therefore `8-rho^2`. Reading the other positive
roots from (10) gives

`s^+(H)=(31+sqrt(13))/2-sqrt(5)-rho^2`.                      (11)

Moreover `rho<sqrt(5)`, since `x^3-4x-1` is negative at 2 and positive at
`sqrt(5)`. Hence

`s^+(H) > (21+sqrt(13))/2-sqrt(5) > 10`;                    (12)

the last inequality follows from `(1+sqrt(13))/2>sqrt(5)`.

The signed Coulson identity is

`s^+(F)-s^-(F)=-(4/pi) integral_0^infinity t Theta_F(t) dt`. (13)

Since `H` has 11 edges, (11)-(13) give the exact majorant integral

`integral_0^infinity t Theta_H(t) dt`
` = (pi/2)(11-s^+(H))`
` = (pi/2)(rho^2+sqrt(5)-(9+sqrt(13))/2) < pi/2`.             (14)

Finally every `G` in the theorem is connected and bicyclic, so it has `n+1`
edges and `s^+(G)+s^-(G)=2n+2`. Combining (1), (13), and (14) yields

`s^+(G)-s^-(G) >= s^+(H)-s^-(H)`

and therefore (3). Numerically, only as orientation and not as part of the
certificate, `s^+(H)=10.5938737512...`; the uniform surplus over `n` is thus
about `0.59387`.
