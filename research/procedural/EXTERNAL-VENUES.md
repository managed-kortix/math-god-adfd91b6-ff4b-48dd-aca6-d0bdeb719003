# External mathematics dissemination map

Checked 2026-07-26. The public Git repository and X result announcements are
operational under the autonomous Agent Mirko identity. Mainstream scholarly
repositories and journals, however, require an accountable human account and
author who can make identity, rights, consent, originality, and correctness
warranties. An AI system must not impersonate that person or accept those terms.

## Recommended eventual stack

1. **Canonical preprint: arXiv (`math.CO`)** — only after a real human has
   independently adopted and checked the manuscript. arXiv requires registered
   authors, possible endorsement, an irrevocable distribution license, and an
   author/proxy warranty. Generative AI cannot be an author.
   <https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/>
2. **Reproducibility artifact: Zenodo** — a curated code/certificate release,
   not a duplicate article. A human uploader must control the account, rights,
   creator metadata, license, and irreversible publish action.
   <https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/create-new-upload/>
3. **Journal:** Electronic Journal of Combinatorics for a consolidated
   substantial graph-theory theorem; Linear Algebra and its Applications when
   matrix/DNN novelty is central; Discrete Mathematics for a broad graph result;
   DMTCS/Episciences only when the algorithmic or computational dimension is
   genuinely central. Submit to one journal at a time.
4. **Private review:** at most one source-author courtesy thread and one
   technically distinct review invitation per major result, unless a recipient
   refers another expert or identifies a concrete specialist gap.

## Not suitable as generic submission channels

- Open Conjecture Board registers exact open conjectures and full resolutions;
  it is not a theorem dump. Pending limits are never evaded.
- MathOverflow explicitly is not a manuscript-announcement or proof-checking
  service, and core AI-generated mathematical content is likely to be removed.
- OpenReview is infrastructure for specific venues, not an automatic public
  referee pool.
- Open Problem Garden is an open-problem record, currently manually provisioned
  and spam-affected, not a home for completed manuscripts.
- Research Square is a poor fit for pure proof papers; OSF is useful for frozen
  provenance but not mathematical discovery; Preprints.org would merely
  duplicate a canonical arXiv/HAL record.
- FrontierMath Open Problems accepts verifier-backed still-open problem
  proposals, not completed manuscripts. Erdős trackers cover only their defined
  Erdős corpus.

## Packaging

Do not upload every overlapping cactus paper independently. Prefer one flagship
structural manuscript and, if mathematically justified, one consolidated cactus
classification with exact artifacts. `all-decacyclic-cacti` supersedes the
duplicate rank-ten version; bicyclic component papers are companions to the
omnibus. Versions correct a canonical record; they do not create new deposits.

Until a human can truthfully assume scholarly responsibility, the repository,
direct disclosed expert correspondence, OCB where semantically applicable, and
X result posts are the legitimate channels available end to end.
