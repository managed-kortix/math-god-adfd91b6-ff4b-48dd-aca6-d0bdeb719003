- **Alternating-sign convention.** Let the original physical path be `x_0=u,x_1,...,x_l=v`, and let `p_j` be its original physical DNN unit vector. Define the transformed chain vector `z_j=(-1)^j p_j`, so `z_j=(-1)^j p_j` and conversely `p_j=(-1)^j z_j`. The payload stores stereographic parameters for `z_1,...,z_(l-1)`, while the verifier supplies `z_0=p_u=branch[u]` and `z_l=(-1)^l p_v=(-1)^l branch[v]`. Hence the physical Gram entry for any two retained marked points `x_i,y_j` (possibly lying internally on different suppressed paths) is exactly `R[x_i,y_j]=<p_i,p'_j>=(-1)^(i+j)<z_i,z'_j>`. On the same path this specializes to `R[x_i,x_j]=(-1)^(i+j)<z_i,z_j>`. Distances `i,j` are measured from each stored path's chosen start endpoint; reversing an entire path requires replacing `i` by `l-i`. In particular, if deletion endpoints `a,b` become suppressed-internal points, locate them as `(path_a,i)` and `(path_b,j)` in the provenance map and compute `r=R[a,b]` by this formula. For an original branch marker use distance zero and its branch vector. Exact excess is `E=sum_(paths) sum_(j=0)^(l-1) (1-<z_j,z_(j+1)>)/(1+<z_j,z_(j+1)>)`; `verify_witness` and `verify_individual_witness` enforce the denominators and `E<=5`. Thus one must never read `<z_i,z'_j>` directly as the physical correlation without the parity factor.

## Fail-closed obstacles

No single callable deletion-to-marked-(Gram,E,r) dispatcher was identified in the audited modules. Kernel/isomorphism, degree-two marker transport, path-occurrence ordering, descendant construction, tree reattachment, and multiblock Gram gluing must be supplied and independently checked. The complete theorem's spectral conclusion does not encode those maps. Analytic/symbolic recipes must be instantiated, not inferred from their theorem tags. Structural owners do not provide Gram data. Complete receipt authentication and child verification remain separate trust roots; this audit only executed the example witness, not full fleet replay. Float objectives and rationalization search functions coexist with exact functions in the engine: use only reconstructed Fraction vectors and exact cost checks. A usable witness produces an upper certificate E (its actual chain excess), not necessarily the globally minimal excess for its branch Gram.

## Explicit rational descendant lift (resolves the construction obstacle above)

A simple exact nonoptimal lift is available: in a transformed chain replace any vertex w by three consecutive copies w,w,w. This adds two steps, both with dot=1 and cost zero, preserving endpoint vectors and total E exactly. Repeating realizes every same-parity descendant with rational vectors. For unambiguous marked transport insert all extra copies immediately after the starting endpoint of each target path, then map each actual physical vertex by its actual distance and alternate signs. This gives an explicit certificate, although it need not optimize the resulting marked r. Thus descendant existence is NOT a mathematical obstacle: what is absent from the existing interface is its implemented, provenance-checked marked export. Original warning about monotonicity alone remains true, but no algebraic equal-angle chains are necessary for a usable exact E,r certificate.
