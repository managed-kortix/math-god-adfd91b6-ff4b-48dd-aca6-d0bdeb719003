# Prompt - all cyclomatic-rank-twelve cacti

## Exact target

Prove that every connected cactus `G` of cyclomatic rank `12` satisfies

```text
s+(G) > |V(G)|.
```

The exact sharp-DNN frontier is already verified and consists precisely of

```text
T^11Q,
T^10PP,
```

where `T=C3`, `P=C5`, and `Q=C_q` for `q>=3`. The all-rank hostile and
nonhostile one-`Q` theorems close `T^11Q`. The live target is therefore a
complete rank-twelve treatment of `T^10PP`, with arbitrary shared cuts, bridge
connectors, and attached trees.

## Victory

Victory means a proof for **all connected rank-twelve cacti**, not merely the
DNN reduction. It must combine the exact frontier with exhaustive structural
coverage of `T^10PP`, assign every physical vertex, shared cut, connector
remnant, and attachment to exactly one induced connected final owner, and use
only proved packet inequalities. Any finite certificate must regenerate its
full universe, reject unknown or malformed cases with `RuntimeError`, and run
byte-identically under normal Python and `python -O`.

## Non-victory

None of the following is victory:

- deriving or rechecking only the DNN frontier `T^11Q,T^10PP`;
- producing a finite census without exhaustive physical final-owner and theorem
  certificates;
- closing only a special endpoint, marked interface family, bouquet, common-cut
  case, or fully shared case;
- extrapolating the rank-eleven census or treating every new residual as a
  bounded-rank terminal;
- using floating-point evidence, qualitative strictness to pay an exact loss,
  shared ownership, or edge-addition monotonicity.

These are progress only. Do not claim rank twelve until every DNN and
`T^10PP` topology is covered.

## Next proof target

Seek the all-rank `T^rPP` theorem. A valid rank-uniform separator/packet proof
would close rank twelve at `r=10` and supersede another growing finite census.
It must preserve cyclic order and interfaces, prove connected induced and
disjoint exhaustive ownership, cover arbitrary connectors and trees, and
terminate only in already proved exact spectral packets. If the all-rank route
fails, compress the obstruction before attempting a rank-twelve census.

No paper, PDF, publication, or public claim belongs to this task.
